## Goal and why this task matters
We are training a ~9B spatial VLM, end to end from RGB, whose chain of thought calls heads inside its own checkpoint and answers from what they print; it must beat open 9B models on ReVSI, VSTI and VSI-Bench and later serve as a cheaper base for navigation fine-tuning. A training leg is one 24-hour Orchard job on 4 H100 that spends about 3.5 h before its first step and then reaches only about 450 steps at about 133 s a step, and the reads that judge each boundary take 4 to 6 h on one GPU slot, so every minute removed buys more steps or more experiments. Four failed discoveries for vehicle N each lost a preparation cycle of about 2.4 h on 4 H100, and the lessons files list defects (missing modules in a child process, paths that differ between the CPU check and the GPU job, lost stderr, silent upload failures) that every lane rediscovers.

## Question
Which launch defects of the last two weeks would a shared harness have caught before a GPU job started, and what is the smallest harness that catches them?

## Method
1. Inventory every launch or discovery defect named in the card and the status log: what failed, at which phase, how long after submission, and the GPU-hours it cost. Write files/B06/DEFECTS.md with one row each and a column saying which harness check would have caught it on Trinity or in a cheap CPU job.
2. Build the harness in your task directory (stdlib Python plus bash, no new dependencies): (a) boot: start the worker with the job's own interpreter and flags (-I -s -B), import its full import closure including every child process's entry module, and fail within seconds, before any staging; (b) one specification that renders both the CPU check job and the GPU job's sbatch headers and environment, with a test that they differ only in declared fields; (c) an EXIT/TERM/USR1 trap that always publishes the exit cause (code, signal, time limit, OOM) and a screened stderr tail to a named sink; (d) a strict upload sink that never reports success without a receipt; (e) a dry-run mode that walks the whole job path with fixtures, including checkpoint, export, stop and resume, as the rehearsal lesson asks.
3. Tests with sbatch, srun and gsutil mocked; at least one planted defect per check.
4. Write files/B06/MIGRATION.md and a diff showing how kit_n_go's job.sh and b/ would call the harness. Do not apply it.
Job names the harness generates must follow a15_cot_ap<opaque hex> with no project, benchmark, dataset, model or method word.

## What counts as a finding, and its price
A finding is a defect class the harness catches, with the past defects it would have caught and their cost. Price as GPU-hours lost per leg in the last two weeks that the harness would have saved. CONFIRMED needs a passing planted-defect test.
