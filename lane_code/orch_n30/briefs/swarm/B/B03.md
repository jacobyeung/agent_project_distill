## Goal and why this task matters
We are training a ~9B spatial VLM, end to end from RGB, whose chain of thought calls heads inside its own checkpoint and answers from what they print; it must beat open 9B models on ReVSI, VSTI and VSI-Bench and later serve as a cheaper base for navigation fine-tuning. A training leg is one 24-hour Orchard job on 4 H100 that spends about 3.5 h before its first step and then reaches only about 450 steps at about 133 s a step, and the reads that judge each boundary take 4 to 6 h on one GPU slot, so every minute removed buys more steps or more experiments. The stop window uploads a 113 GB checkpoint plus a 21.6 GB export at about 90.8 MB/s, and its planned 3,250 s sits only 185 s inside the 3,435 s allowed; every GB removed saves about 11 s of upload at stop and about 8 s of the end-of-job slot copy, and can return training minutes to each leg.

## Question
What exactly do the 113.5 GB checkpoint and the 21.6 GB export (4 x 5,395,758,080 B) hold, by parameter group and state kind (bf16 weights, fp32 masters, AdamW moments, RNG, data cursors), and which bytes are frozen, recomputable or already held by the base model or the init export? Is the 600-s planning reserve per checkpoint and per export (measured writes 56-77 s) idle GPU time or only a budget? Is the step-1 checkpoint needed, given that a node death restarts from the last export with a fresh optimizer?
