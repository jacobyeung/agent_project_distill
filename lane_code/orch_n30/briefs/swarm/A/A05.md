## Goal and why this task matters
We train a ~9B spatial VLM whose chain of thought calls heads inside its own checkpoint and answers from what they print; it must beat open 9B models on ReVSI, VSTI and VSI-Bench. ## Question
Do the conversions from head and tower outputs to printed values follow one consistent convention? Check:
1. Coordinate frames: camera versus world frame, OpenCV versus OpenGL axis order and signs, which frame `f1` names, whether boxes from different frames are moved into one frame before a gap is measured.
2. Pose composition: camera-to-world versus world-to-camera, order of multiplication, quaternion order (wxyz versus xyzw), relative pose between two frames, rotation angle extraction (acos clamp, degrees), the yaw convention stated in the printout versus the one computed.
3. Metric scale: where scale enters depth and pose, whether it is applied once, whether it is applied to translations and depths alike.
4. Depth lifting: intrinsics after resizing or cropping frames, pixel centre convention (0.5 offset), depth units (millimetres versus metres), invalid-depth handling.
5. Box rules: full versus half extents (a half-extent defect in ScanNet++ v1 ground-truth boxes was found on 2026-11-17; look for the same pattern in predicted boxes), yaw applied to extents, the `box_grid_5cm` closest-point gap, the axis-aligned versus oriented box choice.
6. Presence: the threshold and what happens when an object is absent in every frame (does a box still print?).
