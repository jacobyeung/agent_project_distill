## Goal and why this task matters
We train a ~9B spatial VLM whose chain of thought calls heads inside its own checkpoint and answers from what they print; it must beat open 9B models on ReVSI, VSTI and VSI-Bench. Resume decides whether a restarted leg trains the recipe it claims. Another model family (task A01) checks the loss and labels; you give an independent check and own frames and resume.

## Question
1. Frame sampling: how many frames per row, spread versus duplicate temporal pairing (the trained carrier lineage duplicates each of 32 frames into its own temporal pair, about 4,160 visual tokens), and whether training and the evaluation harness (benchmark_eval/frames.py sample_indices, pack_input) build the same layout for the same video.
2. Frame timing: the timestamps or fps passed to the video processor, whether they are the true source times of the sampled frames, and whether frame names in the trace (`f1`, `frames 14,15`) index the same frames the model receives (zero- versus one-based, before or after deduplication).
3. Resume from checkpoint inside the trainer: the step counter, the learning-rate schedule position (50 warm-up steps of 1,000), the sampler position and seed (no row seen twice or skipped), the RNG state, the optimizer state, and the case "restart from the last export with a fresh optimizer": does warm-up restart, does Adam's state start empty with the full learning rate, which rows are replayed?
4. Independent spot check of A01's area: for one synthetic row with an assistant span and a `<tool_response>` block, which tokens carry labels.
