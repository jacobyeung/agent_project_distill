## Goal and why this task matters
We train a ~9B spatial VLM whose chain of thought calls heads inside its own checkpoint and answers from what they print; it must beat open 9B models on ReVSI, VSTI and VSI-Bench. Every training row passes through the converter and dataset builder. A wrong unit, a rounding rule that disagrees with the scorer, a misnamed frame or a development scene leaking into training rows corrupts every leg and every development read built on it.

## Question
Find defects that put a wrong value, a wrong frame or a forbidden scene into a training row. Check:
1. Units and rounding: metres versus centimetres versus feet in questions and answers; rounding of printed numbers versus the precision the scorer (mean relative accuracy, MRA) rewards; integer rendering of counts; negative zero; thousands separators.
2. Frame naming and indexing: zero-based versus one-based frame numbers in traces (`f1`, `frames 14,15`), the mapping from frame name to source video timestamp, and agreement with the evaluation harness's frame sampler (/scratch/jjyeung/swarm_20261120/repo_head/student/landing/student_pilot/benchmark_eval/frames.py).
3. Deduplication: keys used to drop duplicate rows; whether near-duplicates (same scene, same objects, reworded question) survive; whether deduplication can drop the wrong member of a pair.
4. Scene splits and leakage: split.physical_group and every place that excludes development or benchmark scenes. Do two names for one physical room (ScanNet `sceneXXXX_00` versus `_01` rescans; the same room in ScanNet and ScanNet++; a prefix or case difference; ARKitScenes video id versus visit id) compare equal? Feed every distinct development scene name from the corpus through each exclusion function in its plausible training-side spellings and report any that would be admitted.
5. Answer construction: dataset_builder.bare_answer and native_answer for multiple choice (option letter versus option text), and render_target's handling of a missing or empty answer.

## What counts as a finding, and its price
A wrong value, frame or admitted forbidden scene on a concrete input, shown by a test you ran. Price leakage by the number of development items whose scene would be admitted and the points it could inflate on dev-S1+R0-dev; price unit or rounding errors in MRA points on the affected question type (the corpus gives per-type counts); otherwise "unpriced" with the reason.
