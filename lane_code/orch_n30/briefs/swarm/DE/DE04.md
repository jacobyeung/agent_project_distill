## Question
How many rows repeat a question or fact another row already teaches, how many of those repeats disagree on the answer, and does any row come from a development question, room or video?

## Method
1. Check the store; count rows by pool × type.
2. Exact duplicates: identical (scene, normalized question, options, answer); identical trace text within a scene; one qid or source_qid on several rows outside designed twins (a toward row and its away twin share pair_id by design).
3. Near-duplicates with a key per family, within a scene (names lower-cased, articles dropped, a singular rule you state): direction (stand, face, target, facing); relative distance (anchor, candidate set); appearance order (object set); counting (category set); first-seen frame (object); route planning (start, facing, goal); VSTI relations (frame, objects, relation kind); room (scene). Tabulate groups by pool pair: w1g against rows_rebuild_d0 direction rows teaches one triple twice, once without frames and once with them.
4. Conflicts: near-duplicate groups whose answers differ (direction side or quadrant, chosen object, order, count, frame); for counting, compare r1g, b12 and total-count rows of the same scene and category. Conflicting groups rank first.
5. Concentration: rows per scene; the share of rows from the top 1% and 10% of scenes; scenes above 300 rows.
6. Development overlap:
   a. Question ids: normalize development ids (strip devrows:, backdir_, forwardtwin_, room_) and match against qid, source_qid and ids embedded in longer qids (a0_room_vsi590k_058996 holds vsi590k_058996); also match q + 24-hex development ids against weak-pack qids.
   b. Video fingerprints: (dataset family, total_num_frames, fps) equal on both sides marks a candidate same video; frame index sets that are equal or overlap confirm it; when width and height exist on both sides, require them equal. Report candidates and confirmed matches separately with the development set (dev-S1, R0-dev, facing twins, held-out camera, room-134) and the training rows involved.
   c. Names: compare EXCLUSIONS.json's canonical names with development scene and room names after normalization (ScanNet sceneNNNN_00 and _01 are one room; scannetpp and scannetppv2 ids compare equal; ARKit by capture and venue). State how many development scenes no method could compare.
7. Keep files/DE04/dups.csv.gz, conflicts.csv.gz, overlap.csv and one script that reproduces every count; run it twice and confirm identical outputs.
