We train a ~9B spatial VLM whose chain of thought calls heads inside its own checkpoint and answers from what they print; it must beat open 9B models on ReVSI, VSTI and VSI-Bench. The user asked today whether we should collect more reasoning traces; the standing rule is that trace collection scales only where a pilot prices it. The ruling of 2026-11-16 also asks every routed type (absolute distance and the three camera types) to train its other, direct form at about 2,000 rows per type, so direct traces for these types will be written either way. This pilot measures, on real rows, whether a richer qualitative trace can be written from ground-truth facts without frames and checked automatically, how often each style passes, what 1,000 rows cost, and what a training test of the better style would need.

## Question
For the direct form of routed types, written only from a row's ground-truth facts, which of two styles passes automatic fact and grammar checks more often, what does each cost per 1,000 rows when a program writes it and when a frontier model writes it, and what would a training test of the better style need?

## Method
1. Sample 200 rows with random.Random(20261120) over each stratum's sorted i: 60 absolute-distance rows asked in metres (15 per gen_r2 source), 20 units rows (U_Faithful), 40 per camera type. Take all rows of a smaller stratum and say so. Save files/DE05/sample_ids.csv.
2. Extract a fact sheet per row (program): question, options, the row's final answer and its exact format, unit asked; distance rows: per object the frames list, best frame, centre, size and yaw, and the gap; camera rows: from and to frames, dx, dy, dz, distance, turn. Save files/DE05/facts.jsonl.
3. Style M (minimal), rendered by a program for all 200 rows. Distance: one Locate line per object at its best frame, one Step restating what is asked and in which unit, then `</think>` and the answer. Displacement and rotation: one Step restating the frames, the unit or the sign convention, then the answer. Movement direction: one Relate line naming the main direction from dx and dz, one Step mapping it to the option letter, then the answer.
4. Style R (richer), rendered by a program for all 200 rows: style M plus two to four Relate lines from a closed vocabulary of predicates computed from the facts with stated margins. Distance: higher or lower (centre heights differ by more than 0.3 m); much larger or smaller (longest dimensions differ by a factor above 1.5); seen together in frame k (k in both frame lists); left or right of each other, nearer or farther from the starting viewpoint (horizontal centre offsets above 0.5 m). Camera: main and secondary motion words from dx and dz (main above 1.5 times the other), a vertical word only when |dy| exceeds 0.3 m, the turn's side and a band word, with no band word within 5 degrees of a band edge. Record the vocabulary, margins and three wordings per predicate in files/DE05/vocabulary.md.
5. Style R written by you, for 50 of the 200 rows (20 distance including 5 units rows, 10 per camera type): read only the fact sheet, write each trace yourself as a frontier labeller would, and attach a claims list with one entry per Relate line (`{"line": ..., "predicate": ..., "args": [...]}`) in the vocabulary of step 4. Do not generate these with your renderer. Record wall minutes and characters written.
6. One checker for all three arms (files/DE05/check_traces.py): C1 grammar (only Locate, Relate and Step lines; no call tag or `<tool_response>`; numbers only frame indices, counts and question constants; no decimal; no number within 10% of the answer before `</think>`; think non-empty); C2 facts (every Locate frame is in that object's printed frames; every claim's predicate holds under the facts with its margin; the prose line carries its predicate's keywords; every object named is in the fact sheet); C3 answer (the text after `</think>` equals the row's answer, format included); C4 no invented perception (a stated word list). Test the checker on hand-made good and bad traces before using it (files/DE05/test_check.py).
7. Report pass rate per arm × type with Wilson 95% intervals, failure classes with counts and two examples each, and the share of style-R lines that are band words.

## What counts as a finding, and its price

Then write the training test the better style would need: rows (about 2,000 per routed type, per the 2026-11-16 ruling), where they enter (N's routing stream at a boundary, or a branch from an N export), steps (100 to 200; one step is about 133 s on 4 H100, so 100 steps cost about 15 GPU-hours), the read that would show an effect (the direct answer on routed development items when the call is withheld or fails; item counts from CORPUS_MAP.md: dev-S1 distance 150, camera 450, R0-dev distance 580), the smallest paired difference those counts can detect, and the decision rule. End with a one-paragraph answer to "collect more reasoning traces?" for this row kind: scale, do not scale, or test first, with the price.
