## Non-blocking notes
- Lead with `default_orders` (score_k5.py:203-206) when reporting (1). Tower-alone drops the LM's GPU history, so A1 and A2 may already agree. In that case a q1 of "yes" says nothing about the deterministic settings.
- A1 vs dump: the kit rebuilds gravity from row 2 of the dump's camera 0 (build_k5.py:107), and that row is not guaranteed to match the served gravity bit for bit. `unscaled_bit` and `metric_fixed_bit` (score_k5.py:133-137) can therefore read false while the tower matches. K_bit and served_plane_bit are the clean tower check.
- In the reverse arms, the capture-0 recompute comes right after capture 0 (2.py:46). That makes it a weak history test for A2 and D2; the forward arms carry the in-arm test.
- Records name the GPU but not which card (2.py:198). Logging the UUID or PCI bus id would rule out a card change between job 0 and job 1 if D1 and D2 differ.
- REPORT_K5.md:6 does not name three settings: fill_uninitialized_memory (implied by use_deterministic_algorithms), preferred_blas_library, and torch threads=8 (2.py:177). If D1 and D2 differ, the allocator, which stays as served, remains the leading unseparated cause. A follow-up arm with PYTORCH_NO_CUDA_MEMORY_CACHING=1 would isolate it.
- det_factor 1.25 is unmeasured (build_k5.py:47). D runs first in each job, so a slow D arm costs the A arm through the budget skip (0.py:198) and leaves (2) incomplete. The guard still writes receipts.
- o_sub1.sh does not check job 0's final state before pinning to its node (o_sub1.sh:6-8). After a NODE_FAIL, job 1 would sit pending.
