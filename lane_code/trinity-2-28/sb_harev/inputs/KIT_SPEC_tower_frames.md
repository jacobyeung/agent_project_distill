## 1. Question and scope
The reader gives frozen G3T the item's 32 frames and SAM 3 the same 32. K1 priced G3T on 64 dense frames with outputs kept for the 32 anchors. This kit prices, without training: (A) G3T's poses and depth at 32 against 64 input frames; (B) grounding from 64 frames (SAM 3 proposals and lifting on all 64) against 32; (C) the camera calls on dev-S1 at 64 frames and with DA3 poses. The language model, its prompt, its 32 frames and the response format stay as they are: frames outside the model's 32 never appear in a printed frame list, they only add views. This is not lane sb_f64's question (the language model at 64 frames).

## 3. Part A: G3T at 32 against 64 input frames on K1 (about 0.5 GPU-hours; run first)
- **Pass:** G3T on each K1 capture's 32 anchor frames only (296 captures; the reader's input), same settings as the 64-frame pass. Store `raw_pose` and `depth_grid` for the 32.
- **CPU:** (1) poses against GT with `pose_k1.py` unchanged for both inputs (rotation, direction, turn and move proxies), plus two short-baseline bands this kit adds: displacement MRA on anchor pairs with GT displacement from 0.05 to 0.25 m, and rotation MRA on pairs with GT angle from 1 to 5 degrees; (2) depth AbsRel at the anchors against GT rasters, raw and median-aligned, and the per-frame scale log-sd; (3) distance: rebuild the deployed geometry from the 32-frame output with the own-depth scale (the K1 convert step) and price arm A0 and arm A2 of kit `box_ladder_k1` on K1 train.
- **Bar:** "64 input frames pay for pose" if the 64-frame output beats the 32-frame output on the move proxy or on the short-baseline displacement band with a paired capture-bootstrap 95% lower bound above 0 on K1 train captures (5,000 draws, seed 20261119), and the held-out point agrees in sign. "64 input frames pay for lifting" if arm A2 at 64 beats arm A2 at 32 with a paired room-bootstrap lower bound above 0. If neither holds, stop after Part A, report that the tower's input stays at 32, and do not run Parts B and C's 64-frame arms (the DA3 camera arm of Part C still runs).
