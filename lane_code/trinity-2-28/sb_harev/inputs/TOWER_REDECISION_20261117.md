## Question
Lane gt_audit found that K1's ScanNet++ distance labels were built on half-extent boxes and that, with corrected labels, the registered tower rule hands distance from G3T to Pi3X. Is the correction right, which frozen tower should the claimed reader use for `<ground>`/`<gap>` lifting and for `<move>`/`<turn>`, and what should lane reader_r1 do with its step-544 tower-arm read?

## Answer
1. **Keep G3T as the only tower, for distance and camera calls, in leg R1.** Lane reader_r1 runs its tower-arm read as built (G3T, memo on), adds no arm and does not wait. The pin keeps its place on a new basis: on corrected K1 labels no candidate beats the geometry the reader actually serves beyond noise, and Pi3X, the candidate the registered rule now picks, cannot serve camera calls alone, so adopting it would mean carrying two towers.
2. **The correction rule is right.** My independent code reproduces all 1,253 corrected labels (30 of 30 in the stratified sample) and contradicts none of gt_audit's 816 decided boxes. It decides 56 of gt_audit's 88 undecided ScanNet++ boxes, all half, and none full. All 75 ScanNet++ scenes read half (scene median relative extent 0.96 to 0.99), the same four ScanNet scenes read half, and all 159 ARKitScenes scenes read full. 18 held-out and 109 train rows still rest on a box neither reader can decide; on them the GT-geometry arm measures 1.015 times the corrected label at the median (0.917 times the stored one), and dropping them moves the tower comparisons below by at most 0.7 points and changes no verdict.
3. **The registered flip is real but measures the wrong reference.** Under the registered C-rule (reference S4+G1, G3T with a fitted scale) the corrected labels make Pi3X the distance winner: held-out 73.38, +6.44 [+3.42, +10.06]; train +10.00 [+6.80, +13.77]. The flip is not an artefact of the correction: on the 107 held-out rows whose labels never changed, Pi3X still gains +9.16 [+4.06, +15.00] over S4+G1, and the stored-label verdict itself came from the defect: MoGe-2 topped the stored train table by 0.04 points over Pi3X, a lead built on the inflated ScanNet++ rows, where it even beat GT geometry (held-out 68.44 against 61.38). But the reader never serves S4+G1. It serves G3T depth and pose with the own depth head's scale and the own gravity head (GD_reviewed), which scores 75.05 [70.53, 78.89] on corrected held-out distance. Against GD, Pi3X scores −1.67 [−6.08, +2.13] as priced, because the sealed guard refuses 11 held-out rows that GD answers, and +1.32 [−1.22, +4.29] on the 205 rows both answer. DA3 scores −4.03 [−11.03, +1.53] and +1.46 [−1.53, +4.72]. Applied with GD as the reference (unregistered), the same C-rule keeps G3T for distance either way: as priced no candidate qualifies; on rows both answer, Pi3X qualifies but fails the room guard against GD (room train lower bound −5.27), and the rule does not fall through to DA3.
4. **Camera: Pi3X cannot take `<turn>`; DA3 could.** On K1's 32 anchors, each scored against frame 1 (279 captures), Pi3X has a lower median pair error than G3T but fails whole ARKitScenes captures: 23 of 144 have a median rotation error above 15 degrees, against 9 for G3T and 6 for DA3. A turn proxy (rotation-angle MRA) therefore falls by 2.29 [−4.09, −0.67] against G3T, while a move proxy (metric displacement MRA) rises by 2.08 [+0.56, +3.53]. DA3 beats G3T on both, +1.02 [+0.20, +1.94] and +3.26 [+2.09, +4.49], with no tail; on the 51 held-out captures both differences lie within noise. No cached output prices Pi3X or DA3 on the dev-S1 camera types, where G3T scores 82.3 / 94.7 / 75.7 (job 170056). One offline camera-program read on the 450 routed camera items would settle it, at about 0.5 H100-hours per tower (guess).
5. **If a tower replaces G3T later, DA3 should replace it alone.** DA3 ties Pi3X on distance (train +0.89 [−1.66, +3.41] in Pi3X's favour; DA3 wins the registered rule on the unaffected rows), it has the best K1 poses, and it wins room against both references (against GD: train +6.36 [+1.18, +11.87], held-out +5.00 [−3.60, +14.78]). As a single tower it adds 0.14B parameters, and the checkpoint stays "about 11B". Pi3X would need G3T beside it for camera, a 12.5B checkpoint, for a distance gain inside noise. DA3 earns a switch only after its dev-S1 camera read and a K1 pricing under the reviewed guard (Tests).
6. **What the choice is worth.** On K1 held-out distance, switching from G3T to Pi3X is worth −1.7 task points as priced and +1.3 on rows both answer; switching to DA3 is worth −4.0 and +1.5. In VSI-Bench eight-task value that is at most +0.2 points. As an upside scenario only, if every ReVSI distance item routed through the tower and the K1 gain carried over with predicted masks, it would be worth +0.19 (Pi3X) or +0.21 (DA3) ReVSI points, with upper bounds +0.61 and +0.67 (distance is one of seven categories). The registered +6.44 against S4+G1 would be worth +0.92 ReVSI points only to a reader that served S4+G1, and ours does not.

## Options rejected and why
- **Following the registered verdict literally (Pi3X for distance, DA3 for room, G3T for camera).** Three frozen towers (about 13.9B) for differences that vanish against the geometry the reader serves; room has no call in the claimed reader (NEXT_LEG_DECISION_20261117.md).
- **Pi3X for distance beside G3T for camera.** +1.36B parameters and a second in-call tower for a held-out gain of −1.7 / +1.3.
- **Pi3X for both.** It fails whole ARKitScenes captures on rotation (23 of 144), which `<turn>` would inherit.
- **DA3 for both, now.** Its distance gain lies within noise, its dev-S1 camera accuracy is unmeasured against G3T's 82.3 / 94.7 / 75.7, the reader has no DA3 path, the selector head was trained on G3T-geometry features, and a switch re-reads step 544's baselines.
- **Pricing Pi3X and DA3 under the reviewed guard in this lane.** It would replace the refusal-neutral estimate with measured rows on 21 captures, but neither verdict turns on it: under GD as reference the room guard decides distance, and room carries no refusals.

## What might not work and how we would notice
- **K1 distance uses GT masks.** With SAM 3 masks the geometry gap may shrink or grow; reader_r1's paired read and a later DA3 arm measure it on dev-S1.
- **The refusal-neutral comparison assumes the refused captures behave like the rest.** They are hard rows (GD 57.3 on Pi3X's 11 against 76.0 elsewhere). Test 1 measures them.
- **The camera proxies are not the dev-S1 types.** Test 2 measures the types.
- **GD's scale belongs to step 544's own depth head.** If R1 training moves that head, the tower arm's distance quality moves with it; R1's milestone reads of the tower arm show it.
- **The 18 held-out rows on doubly undecided boxes could hold a full box.** A GT-arm ratio near 2 would show it; it sits at 1.015 there and dropping the rows changes nothing.
