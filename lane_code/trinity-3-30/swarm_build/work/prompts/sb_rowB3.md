Spec (orchestrator, binding): for each queried object the trace states whether it is in view at frame k and reasons from where it was last seen; never "located in frame k" for an object out of view. Frame numbers are indices 1..32, "of 32" at first mention. Do not remove rows whose objects are all out of view.
Balance: answer option position uniform per family (dev step 200: last option chosen 54/64/100 of 150 against 37/50/75 truths; Right 110 vs 79 truths; Up 92 vs 76; Far 94 vs 86) and the answer against first-seen rank (the earliest first-seen object is right on 652 of 1,500 sampled training rows; the model picks it on 216 of 450 dev items). Report histograms before and after.
