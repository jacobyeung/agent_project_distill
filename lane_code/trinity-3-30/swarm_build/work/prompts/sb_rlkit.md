## Background
A ~9B spatial VLM (Qwen3.5-9B base) is trained by SFT (leg R1 on the older tree P; N is the rebuilt tree that will train the next leg from P's export) to write a chain of thought that places routed calls to heads inside its own checkpoint (camera pose via frozen G3T, object distance via grounding + depth, room size answered directly) and answers from the printed responses. The RL stage comes after SFT. Reward = correct answer (the benchmark scorers: MRA for numeric, exact for choice), plus a short chain of thought, plus a call placed where the question type is routed (and no call where it is not). Training rooms only: never R0-dev, the 80 withheld rooms, dev-S1 or benchmark scenes (N's room rule student_pilot/r1_rooms.py and its pinned lists enforce this; reuse them).
