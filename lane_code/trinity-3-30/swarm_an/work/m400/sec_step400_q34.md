
### S400.3 Relative size: an unstable trace form, near-equal objects flip (`relsize.py`, `relsize2.py`, `v7phr.py`)
- **Scores** (dev-S1, 150): 91.3 / 88.7 / 90.0 / 87.3 at steps 544 / 100 / 200 / 400. Between 200 and 400, 8 items go right to wrong and 4 wrong to right; between 544 and 400, 14 and 8. 117 items are right and 3 wrong at all four steps.
- **The trace form is not stable at R1's steps.** Step 544 writes one form on 150 of 150 items ("measure their 3D boxes' longest edges", two lengths, the comparison; 13 wrong). At step 400 the traces split into four forms: two stated lengths ("the longest edge of X is 33 cm; ... of Y is 45 cm") 60 (6 wrong); a bare comparison with no number ("the window is longer than the table") 61 (12 wrong); an order without numbers ("order them from shortest to longest") 18 (0 wrong); the relative-distance order ("the candidates ordered nearest to farthest from ...", "The question asks for the closest") 7 (1 wrong); other 4. At step 200 the same forms hold 27, 50, 55, 3 and 15.
- **The flips are near-equal objects.** Of the 8 right-to-wrong items since step 200, 5 state lengths that now invert a small gap (trash bin 1.17 m against window 1.24 m, where step 200 wrote 1.33 against 1.11; slippers 32 cm against shoes 30 cm; bucket 36 cm against trash can 34 cm), and 3 are bare comparisons. Since step 544, 8 of the 14 losses are bare comparisons and 5 state lengths.
- **Row families.** Store v7 holds no relative-size row (its only size family is object_size_estimation, 9,778 rows) and no row writes "the longest edge of", "is shorter than", "is longer than", "compare their" or "order them from shortest". The one borrowed template is relative distance's: "ordered nearest to farthest" appears in 5,260 object_rel_distance rows (and 5,932 rows whose category my regex did not read) and "asks for the closest" in 10,382; it reaches 7 size traces at step 400 (3 at step 200). R1 added no family that teaches a size comparison, so no added family can be the cause by its content; the loss follows from the base's form fading without rows to hold it, and the 4-point fall is 4 net items (95% interval from the decision read: -9.55 to +1.03).
- **What would settle it:** a few hundred relative-size rows in the two-length form from ground-truth boxes would fix the form; whether it recovers the 8 items needs the boxes of the flipped items, which the dev set does not carry.
