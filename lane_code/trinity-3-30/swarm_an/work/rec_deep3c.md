### 10.24 Toward direction on the ReVSI-style rooms, every wrong trace read (Devin `san_dd_dirtw`; its classes recounted by me)
- **Classes of the 115 wrong toward items at step 200** (one class per item): boundary 49 (step 544's angle within 25 degrees of the line between the truth's region and the answer's), mirrored 43 (side flipped, front and back kept, not a boundary case), front-back 18, opposite quadrant 5. The 69 items wrong at every step hold 25, 27, 12 and 5 and cost 1.31 ReVSI points (17 easy, 28 hard; the 24 medium items have no leaf); 2.15 is the price of all 74 easy and hard toward errors.
- **The boundary test is weak evidence:** step 544's angle lies within 25 degrees of a boundary of the truth's region on 50 of 115 wrong items and 95 of 261 right ones (43% against 36%). The angle comes from a small vocabulary (66 distinct values; seven of them cover 198 of 376 items), so "near the boundary" means that the base placed the target slightly to one side. The mirrored and opposite-quadrant errors are confident: 22 of the 27 always-wrong mirrored items carry an angle 43 to 117 degrees from the separating line.
- No exchange of roles explains the errors: hard wrong answers match "faced object and target exchanged" on 25 of 46, against 22.6 expected from the model's own answer frequencies.
- **Neither object size nor name frequency separates wrong from right.** R0-dev accuracy is 69.9% (156 items) when a name occurs in none of the 93,370 direction rows, 69.0% (171) at 1 to 99 rows and 69.4% (49) at 100 or more; dev-S1 items with fully familiar names score 88.5% (407), so R0-dev trails by 19 points at equal vocabulary. Smallest object under 30 cm: 66% (121 items); 30 to 100 cm: 69% (93).
- **The rooms matter across types for direction.** The 69 fall in 47 of 97 rooms (no excess concentration, p = 0.28), but rooms with two or more of them score lower on every other type: away 51.5% against 67.6%, relative distance 62.7% against 73.5%, appearance order 52.0% against 68.5%, counting 43.2% against 57.5%. Per room, toward accuracy correlates with away (0.33), relative distance (0.22) and appearance order (0.19). Side errors cluster: 9 of 79 rooms get every side judgement but one wrong, against 4.8 under shuffling (p = 0.021). With six direction items per room a "consistent but wrong layout" cannot be told from noise (random answers fit a layout on 704 of 776 draws).
- **Why R0-dev and not dev-S1:** video length (median 136 against 132 s), frame spacing, wording, the share of back truths and step 544's angle distribution are matched; dev-S1's rooms are held out too (none of its 27 ScanNet++ rooms is among the 560 training scenes of the size rows). What differs is the rooms' source (R0-dev rooms carry no dataset tag) and the label vocabulary (an object absent from a 2,000-row sample on 293 of 376 R0-dev items against 24 of 440). By elimination, an inference, the gap sits in the rooms or in how R0-dev's labels are computed; R0-dev trails dev-S1 by 18 points on toward direction and by 6 on relative distance, so part of it is specific to direction.
- **Evidence-bearing forms:** Locate lines before the relation exist in the "Seen:" pools and would move a few items at most (the 45 toward items that open with "Seen:" were already easier at every step); in those traces 356 of 599 cited frame numbers exceed 32. A bearing from three `<ground>` centres is the only form that adds information, and the trace ruling keeps direction direct because programs lost 10.0 to 27.3 direction points with deployed masks; a ground response in the routed read prints a bed of 0.55 x 0.10 x 0.22 m, so its centres are suspect.
