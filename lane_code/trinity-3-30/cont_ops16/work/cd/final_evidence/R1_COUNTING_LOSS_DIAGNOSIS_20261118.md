# Why leg R1 loses object counting at step 100, and the fix for the step-200 decision (count_diag, 2026-11-18)

**Question.** Why does R1's step-100 export (run 673bc60f, read 172466) lose object counting against step 544 (dev-S1 -8.27 [-14.94, -2.13], R0-dev -4.10 [-8.55, +0.39], pooled -5.27, the registered rule would stop), when D0 m100 and H1-1 m100 gained on the same items? Which row family causes it, what happens on dev-S1 hard direction, and what store or mix change fixes it for a relaunch from step 544 and for a continuation from R1's step 200?

**Answer.** The loss comes from the trace form of store v7's single-category counting rows. In 8.36 of its 10.87 counting rows per step, R1 trains a first-seen list ("locate each distinct X at its first appearance: frame 1; frame 1; ..."): rows_a2's 6,552 retraced D0 rows plus D0's 84 ground-truth lists (6.39 per step) and b12's single rows (1.97 per step). D0, whose kit and store v5 H1-1 also ran, drew this form at 0.07 rows per step and drew the procedure-only form ("find each distinct X, across the frames, and count it once") at 7.73. Half of the training lists repeat an entry, because several instances often first appear in the same frame. R1 m100 writes the list on 137 of 150 dev-S1 items and 246 of 287 R0-dev single items. On 28 of 150 and 68 of 287 it repeats one entry until the 16,384-token cap, usually from the first entry. In the scored B2048 arm the forced close then answers a large number (median 38). These runaways carry -6.53 of dev-S1's -8.27 and -6.62 of R0-dev's -4.10; with runaway items scored as step 544, R1's counting change is -1.73 [-8.07, +4.30] and +2.52 [-1.06, +6.14]. Lists that repeat an entry without looping over-count as well ("frame 24; frame 24; frame 24; frame 24" gives 4 for 2 sockets). D0 and H1-1 show the same mechanism where they trained the list: their only list rows were totals, and they loop on 34 and 39 of the 98 multi-category items and on 0 of 437 single items. The fix removes the list form from counting: a REMOVE list for a continuation, and restored D0 v5 targets plus a REMOVE list for a relaunch. The dev-S1 hard-direction loss is not an "away means back" prior: R1 answers back less often than step 544 and loses on back-truth items. The facing twins tie front and back to the question's wording.

## Options rejected
- **Cap the first-seen list at a small instance count.** The repeat that starts the loop sits in short lists too (1,256 of 3,877 r1g 2-entry lists read "frame k; frame k"), and 18 of 28 dev-S1 loops start at the first entry, so a cap keeps the pattern.
- **Take out only b12's families (totals and b12 counting).** rows_a2's retrace supplies 6.39 of the 8.36 list rows per step, and all 137 dev-S1 lists use its phrasings.
- **Lower the counting dose and keep the form.** D0 changed its size template within about 820 rows, so even 2 list rows per step reach that by about step 400.
- **Keep lists without a repeated entry (strictly increasing frames).** This removes the repeated token pattern, but it is unmeasured. It also biases the rows toward small counts (97% of 6-or-more lists repeat), which teaches the model to miss instances that share a first frame. It is a candidate for a later dose read, not for step 200.
- **Restore step 544's detect-and-match form.** No admitted store carries it. D0's rows lane dropped the sealed rows' frame citations because they disagreed with ground truth on about a third of the checkable cases (D0 card, item 8).

## What might not work, and how we notice
- The continuation may unlearn 200 steps of the list form slowly. Notice: m400's single-item runaway count (m100: 28 of 150, 68 of 287) should fall toward D0's 0.
- Multi-category accuracy without totals rows is unmeasured (98 R0-dev items, 18% of the pooled counting statistic; step 544 31.4). Notice: multi-item MRA and loops at the next read.
- The procedure-only form under-counts (D0 m100: 54 under, 16 over on dev-S1; true 3 -14.62 [-26.00, -5.19]). Notice: deltas at true count 3 or more.
- The rows reviewer judged the procedure-only form answer-only in substance, so the trace carries no evidence; accepting it for R1 is an orchestrator decision.
- The kit may refuse the new reason string `count_diag_first_seen_list_form`; r1_ops5 checks it, and matching is by row_key.
- Dev-S1 hard direction may keep losing back-truth items while the facing cell ties side to wording. Notice: back answers among the 150 hard items (m100: 23, truth 40). This diagnosis proposes no direction change.
