The trace ruling is final. I adopted it, and three builds are now applying it.

**The ruling**
- **Programs only for five types:** absolute distance, room size and the three camera types. Direction and counting stay direct, and a prompt flag is rejected.
- **Direct rows get a qualitative trace:** each object located by one frame, relations in words, the question's own steps, explicit sums. A trace is never empty and never states a length, angle or coordinate that nothing printed.
- **Routed rows train on deployed responses:** absolute-distance and room rows take responses from the pipeline that runs at inference, on training scenes. Rows where it agrees with ground truth are kept, as are rows where it fails and the trace then answers directly. Silently wrong rows are left out, and no row ends in a refusal.
- **Facing-away defect:** fixed without tools, by paired toward/away rows whose trace carries the half turn as an explicit step. Step 544 currently gives the forward answer on 74 of 150 away questions.
- **Falsifying reads:** four tests on dev-S1 at step 200, with thresholds, are registered in the record.

**What it changes**
- The ruling rewrites 121.5 of the 192 language rows per step in the last leg.
- The first-pass integration tree would have supervised tool calls inside direct rows; its second pass now refuses them.

**Launched (Codex Astra)**
- `rs_rows` second pass: a grammar validator, its four families re-rendered, and the last leg's breaching direct families re-rendered.
- `rs_tree` second pass: the contract, row intake, the corrected patches, and the pre-launch card.
- `o3_cache`: a CPU study of whether a tool response reveals its own errors, plus a kit to render deployed responses on about 2,189 training scenes. The ruling estimates that at 20 to 32 GPU-hours and about 20 GB of frames to stage. Nothing is launched.

**Two points are yours; builds proceed on the defaults**
1. May a direct trace cite one frame per object taken from ground-truth visibility? Default: yes, one first-seen or best-view frame, never an exhaustive list.
2. Are 3D-box label rows perception supervision or question-answer rows under the trace rule? Default: keep them with one locate line; the alternative is to drop the family.
