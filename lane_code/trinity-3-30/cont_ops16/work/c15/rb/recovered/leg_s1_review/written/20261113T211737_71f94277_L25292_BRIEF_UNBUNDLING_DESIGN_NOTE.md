# Research and design note: what "Unbundling Multimodal Vision Encoding" means for our spatial VLM

The user pointed us to https://unbundling.github.io/ ("Unbundling Multimodal Vision Encoding", Brown, Hoang, Qian, Li, Lewis, Ghazvininejad, Zettlemoyer, Beyer, Ren, Verbeek, Xie, Xu; Meta FAIR and NYU; arXiv 2611.17334) with the words "this might be useful". ## What the project page says (verify against the paper; do not trust this summary)

A pretrained vision encoder bundles three choices: a visual prior from separate pretraining, dedicated vision capacity (parameters only vision tokens use), and pre-fusion placement. Training everything from scratch at matched lifetime compute (backbones of 1.7B to 6.4B; a 0.4B, 27-layer ViT), the authors report that the prior is optional, that dedicated capacity matters (an encoder-free model trails by 4 to 7 points of average VQA and needs about three times the compute), and that the capacity can sit inside the backbone: "in-fusion" vision-only modules (attention projections and an FFN branch for vision tokens, zero-initialized) in the first eight layers match or beat the separate encoder (45.0 against 44.4; 50.1 against 48.6 after mid-training) with a shorter time to first token (37 ms against 64 ms). Images only (224 and 448 px), Cambrian-7M instruction tuning, 16 Cambrian VQA benchmarks; no video, no spatial benchmark, no code release stated.
