## Weaknesses found

- **Facing away:** when a direction question says "facing in the opposite direction of X", both checkpoints answer as if facing X; 26 of 33 ReVSI answers are the label rotated 180°. No training row had that wording. We built 33,985 ground-truth rows that teach the reversal, plus matched direct-answer rows; an independent review recomputed every label and admitted the set.
- **Room size:** CP1's answers run 1.5 times the label, and its reasoning quotes an old room definition. The next run's room rows use re-rendered labels.
- **Route planning:** CP1 scores 26 against f17's 58 on VSI-500, because its training had no route-planning rows. The next run adds 9,404 direct ones.
- **Counting form (T0, thinking off):** it answers "2 fridges and 2 desks" where the question asks for a total, and the official extractor cannot parse it.
