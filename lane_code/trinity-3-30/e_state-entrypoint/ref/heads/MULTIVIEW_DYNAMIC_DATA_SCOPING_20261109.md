# Training data for multi-view (MindCube) and dynamic (DSI-Bench, ReSTI) benchmarks: scoping (streams2, 2026-11-09)

**Question (orchestrator 0443Z).** Which public training sets match MindCube, DSI-Bench and ReSTI? Which test items must we exclude? What can our own GT generate cheaply? What could enter CP3 (Wed) or the decay run?

**Answer.** Of the three, only MindCube ships a training split (about 10K questions). DSI-Bench and ReSTI are test-only, so dynamic skill must come from other sets: SAT (48K synthetic dynamic QA) and MultiSPA's object-movement engine. For CP3 the cheapest correct addition is our own GT multi-view rows on A3 ScanNet clips (CPU, about one day, ~30-45K rows). MindCube's train split is the second addition (~10K rows, half a day). ReSTI's ScanNet rooms sit inside our training streams, so ReSTI's 783 ScanNet questions cannot be read as held out for the student.

## 2. Test-split and venue-sibling exclusion
- **BENCH_SCENE_EXCLUSIONS_v2** lists 351 native scenes for these three benchmarks: ReSTI 325 ScanNet scans in 150 rooms (scene0000-0149 with siblings), MindCube 24 (5 ScanNet scenes and 4 ARKit captures of the tinybench, plus siblings), and both 2. DSI has 0 rooms in our corpora (internet video).
- **Overlap with our streams today** (`streams2/work/flt/sib_mvdyn.py`; tokens in `BAD_TOKENS_MVDYN.json`). The A1 division train holds 187 of these scenes and A3 holds 331.
  - Rows hit: pose A3 22,160, A2-new 4,880; pointing pa3 22,160, pa3b 22,160, b2/b2a 2,944; VQA x4 5,824, a.jsonl 1,715, Part C 5,481, box 602.
  - Nearly all hits are ReSTI ScanNet rooms, which are ScanNet training scans. Under USER 11-08, public training-split scenes stay in training. So the student's ReSTI score is reportable only on its 999 Waymo + Omni6DPose questions, unless we drop those rooms (~10% of A3 pose and pointing rows).
- **MindCube.** The tinybench groups (ids in `MindCube_tinybench.jsonl`) must be excluded by group id, and by image hash for the self-collected and hash-named "among/around" groups that no scene id covers (v2 `not_derivable`). Training questions on the 9 tinybench scenes must drop too.
- **SPAR-7M, MultiSPA.** Filter by scene id against the full v2 list (VSI-Bench, VSTI, ReVSI, ReSTI, MindCube) plus our held-out banks, with the same token join as `flt/sib.py`.
