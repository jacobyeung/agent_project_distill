# Drastic architecture for pose, depth and pointing (judge of 5 designs, 2026-11-09 08:40Z)

## Question
Every LM-carrier pose variant (f17, C1-a, T0, T1, the loss and layout arms) sits at 18-20 deg translation direction and 46-49% centre error, while G3T alone reads 3.65 deg / 8.7% fitted centre / 4.0 deg rotation on the same 312 scenes. Clicks are the routing bottleneck (58-61% in-mask, 36-74% null-on-absent; GT clicks make routing beat direct by 15-31 points). The user is open to drastic architecture changes and other ~9-13B bases (27B only if necessary), accepts 64/96 frames and G3T inside the model (fine-tuned preferred, frozen if better), and requires one end-to-end model with RGB video in. Which of the five designs (GF, MDG-3D, L3D, TriTower-9B, VSA-PTR) becomes the CP2/CP3 target, which ONE decisive experiment builds now beside GX/GH0/F64, and what do the running experiments decide?

## Answer
- **Target architecture: GF+T, "Geometry-First Qwen with towers".** Keep the Qwen3.5-9B LM and ViT (T0/CP1 lineage). Put G3T inside the forward at 64-96 frames and read pose, depth and point maps from its own heads through fixed layers with a metric scale (V-C/G3T ratio first, learned scale token second). Give the LM two click sources behind one `<ground>` call: SAM 3 as the pretrained grounding tower now, and GF's pointer head (GH0 -> GHG with G keys) trained in parallel as the own-head path; the source with higher held-out accepted precision routes. A fixed 3D aggregation layer, mask-derived extents and closest points, and a `<geo>`/`<calc>` read layer inside `generate()` turn these into text. Implicit channels (GXC carrier, GeoAlign-style patch connector, 3D position embedding) enter by stacking after the GX read, zero-init, guarded by VSI-500. No base swap: the limits are interfaces, not LM capacity. ~11.4B parameters at inference.
- **The one decisive experiment: TOWER-0**, an inference-only read today that merges TriTower's BX-0 with GF's pose cells and L3D's Gate 0: G3T at 64-96 frames with the V-C scale on the harness, dev pool and ARKit bank; SAM 3 clicks and masks on the pointing sets; a click-source x geometry-source factorial through the unchanged SPEC programs. ~24 GPU-h, zero training, read by ~01:00Z 11-10 at the latest. It measures the common geometry ceiling every design shares and tests the strongest prior for the binding constraint (clicks).
- **Build-first second kit:** GF-0's GHG (GH0 plus a zero-init G key stream) and the shared aggregation module, launched on GH0's GPUs after GH0's read. MDG-A is deferred: SAM 3 masks give the extents, closest points and presence it was designed for, at zero training cost. VSA-PTR and the LM-swap screen close, except a cheap Qwen3-VL-8B pointing read.
- Status: TOWER-0 read PARTIAL-1 (lane ttower, 11-09 13:20Z). P passes: G3T x s at 64 frames reads direction 3.61 deg, rotation 2.98 deg and raw centre 10.6% on the 312 ScanNet scenes. It reads 3.25 deg on unseen ScanNet scans_test and 5.48 deg on the ARKit bank, and its own gravity head errs by 2.2 deg. With its own V-C scale, raw centre is 11.1% on scans_test and 25.2% on ARKit, on the 25% bar, because T0's depth head reads ARKit depth 21% long. Depth AbsRel is 0.063, against V-C's 0.118. G fails. Zero-shot SAM 3 reaches in-mask 74.0%, null 66.0% and scene-absent FP 14.7%. After A1 it reaches 67.6% / 79.7% / 9.0%. The GH0-gated hybrid reaches 60.6% / 91.9%, and the 3D-aggregated row reaches precision 67.5% at recall 58.6%. R passes only in two cells. With GT clicks x G3T x s, routing gains +16.5 / +16.1 / +11.6 over f17 on direction medium / hard / relative distance. On the camera types it gains +30 / +50 on VSTI-450 and +34 / +49 / +65 on the clean pool. Zero-shot SAM 3 masks with abstain and fallback beat f17 on 2 of 4 types, A1 masks on 0 of 4, and no own click source beats f17 on 3. Action: adopt G3T x s for pose, depth and camera-type decoding now; pointing needs a trained own head.

## Reasons with evidence
- **Pose is solved by reading G3T, not by training the LM to read it.** Four of five designs read G3T's camera head through a fixed layer; the fifth (VSA-PTR) transplants G3T onto Qwen-ViT tokens and falls back to G3T inside if the transplant fails. G3T's 3.65 deg / 8.7% beats every LM path, and Cambrian-P shows the same wall at scale (LM-read ATE 0.078 vs VGGT 0.035). The remaining pose question is metric scale: G3T reads 0.46x GT; the V-C ratio needs no parameters, and PDG v3's learned scale branch reached median 0.976 (pose-implied 0.992, 97% within +-25%). GX decides only whether the LM path also helps direct answers and whether G3T may take LM-path gradients.
- **Clicks bind, and presence binds clicks.** Test 1's size fix recovered +2-4; false positives dominate (T0 points at 66% of absent objects). Every surviving design therefore carries an explicit presence decision: SAM 3's presence token (IL_MCC 0.77 -> 0.82 with the head; 0.44 -> 0.68 with 30 hard negatives), GH0/GHG's per-frame presence, MDG's global x frame product. SAM 3 is the only source readable without training: SA-Co/Gold cgF1 54.1 against OWLv2 24.6; LVIS zero-shot mask AP 48.5; 30 ms per image on an H200 ([SAM 3](https://arxiv.org/html/2511.16719v2)). Its indoor ScanNet-frame behaviour is unmeasured, which is what TOWER-0 reads.
- **Masks beat clicks for distance and size.** A single interior GT click caps abs distance at 31.3; edge clicks reach 56.9; a GT mask plus GT depth box reaches size MRA 84.1 against the native box's 66.7 (MDG's evidence). SAM 3 and MDG both give masks; SAM 3 gives them today.
- **The LM is not the limit.** Zero-shot VSI across 8-9B bases spans 54-58 (Qwen3.5-9B 54.3, InternVL3.5-8B 56.1, Qwen3-VL-8B 57.9); Qwen3.6-27B reads 59.8. After spatial SFT, Qwen3.5-9B reaches 70.2 (SpatialAxiom-9B), within ~3 points of the best 8-35B fine-tunes. Qwen3.5-9B leads its class on grounding (RefCOCO 89.7). A swap costs 2-3 days of port plus a T0-length rerun (~400-800 GPU-h) and discards the T0/C1-a warm starts, with ~10 days left. Only Qwen3-VL-8B ports in ~8 h (the trainer already registers a Qwen3-VL student).
- **Geometry input helps direct answers when it enters before the LM as latent features.** GeoAlign 71.4 vs 66.8 (pre-LLM injection beats mid and late), SpaceMind 69.6 vs 63.1, GeoThinker 72.6 on Qwen3-VL-8B ([GeoAlign](https://arxiv.org/html/2604.12630v1), [SpaceMind](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2511.23075v1), [GeoThinker](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2602.06037)). Explicit 3D position embeddings are weaker evidence: VG-LLM's predicted point maps gave 31.7 vs 31.9, Video-3D LLM's PE moved ScanQA EM by +0.06, and Qwen-3D's vanilla 3D PE lost 3.3. So the latent connector (GF 6b) ranks above L3D's P3E, and both enter by stacking after the GX read, not in the decisive test.
- **Calendar.** AWS stops 15:30-20:00Z today and CP1 holds all 32 from 20:00Z; Orchard's world-4 slot is ~22:45Z after a ~120 GB upload. Only an inference-only test can read today; every training test reads 11-10 at the earliest. TOWER-0's GPU legs run on evalx's share before 15:20Z.
