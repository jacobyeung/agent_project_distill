# D0 stop rule and launch conditions (registered 2026-11-16T03:35:28Z, before submission)

**Question.** When does the D0 leg (sealed LEG-S1 trainer, row store v5 4af43b40, warm start from the step-544 export, 1,000 steps, 4 GPUs on Orchard) stop early, and what cancels it before it starts?

**Rule.** (Items 1 to 3 as amended 2026-11-16T09:40:26Z, before any D0 export had been read.) D0 is read at steps 100, 200, 400, 600 and 800 and on its final export, against step 544 with the same reader on the same items (paired differences, room bootstrap). F1 to F4 are the tests of TRACE_RULING section 2.7; the F1 types are route planning, counting, direction easy, medium and hard, relative distance and object size, and nothing else enters a stop decision.
1. F1 fails at step 200 or at any later read (a registered direct type has its paired difference wholly below zero): the leg stops.
2. Facing twins, by point estimates of away accuracy at the three difficulty levels (step 544: 38 / 20 / 6): at step 200 the leg stops if away accuracy is not above step 544's on all three levels. At steps 400, 600 and 800 it stops if away accuracy fell below the previous read's on at least two of the three levels while F2 has not passed. Step 100 gives no verdict.
3. F2 (lower bound of away accuracy above 54 / 40 / 36, at most 20% of pairs answered alike, toward accuracy not below zero in its interval; the point-estimate reading printed beside it) is the test of the facing fix. It is reported at every read and decided on the final export; by itself it stops nothing. F3 and F4 are reported and stop nothing.
4. Before the first update: admission refusal and the native first-step gate on all ranks stop the job. Steps 1 to 3 carry a gross-failure alarm (ruled 2026-11-16T07:40Z, before the first update existed): pose and pointing alarm if non-finite or above 1.5 times the maximum of step 544's steps 530 to 544 on two consecutive steps; VQA, whose rows all changed trace form, alarms if non-finite, above 1.5 times the VQA loss the CPU proof's stage 2 measured on the first batch, or rising by more than 50% from step 1 to step 3; routing is exempt (opener rows re-initialized). A value outside step 544's MAD bounds but under these limits is a warning, not a cancel. An alarm cancels the job.
5. The CPU proof (Orchard job 170109): a stage-1 failure would have cancelled the job before start; stage 1 passed. Job 170111 started at 07:24:54Z, before stage 2 ended, so a stage-2 failure on a measured rank is reported to the orchestrator as a blocker and the job's own first-step gate decides. A rank that stage 2 does not finish counts as not measured.

**Reasons.** The leg costs about 146 GPU-hours; step 200 costs about 30. A regression on a direct type means the re-rendered rows need repair, which more steps on the same rows cannot give. With 50 twins per level, F2's lower-bound reading needs point estimates near 68 / 56 / 52, which a leg starting from 38 / 20 / 6 will almost surely miss at step 200 even if the fix works; a rule that stops an improving leg defeats its purpose, so the stop decision rests on whether away accuracy moves, and F2 judges the fix at the end.

**Rejected.** Stop on any F2 failure at step 200: with 150 twins a rising but not yet passing away accuracy would be cut off. No stop rule: breaches the gate for runs above 64 GPU-hours.

**What might be wrong, and how we would notice.** The step-100 and step-200 reads carry the warm-start confound (fresh optimizer, restarted schedule), so a dip at step 100 that recovers by 200 is expected; the rule reads step 200. The facing thresholds rest on 150 twins; the report prints intervals. The 19,146 procedure-only counting, size and appearance rows may pull those types down; F1 on counting and size shows it.
