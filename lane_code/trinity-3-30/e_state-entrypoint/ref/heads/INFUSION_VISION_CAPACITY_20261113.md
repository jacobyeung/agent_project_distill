## Question
Does the finding that dedicated vision-only capacity can sit inside the early backbone layers ("in-fusion") change what we build next for the 9B student, in particular for cross-frame geometry (pose, depth, correspondence) and grounding from the model's own vision pathway?

## Answer and recommendation
1. The paper shows that a multimodal model needs parameters used only by vision tokens, and that zero-initialized vision-only branches in layers 1-8 of the backbone match a separate encoder; it shows this for single images, trained from scratch or midtrained from a 1.7B text model, with VQA and caption loss as the only measures. Nothing in it tests video, geometry, a pretrained VLM, or a backbone above 6.4B.
2. It transfers to us as one testable hypothesis: our student has no trainable vision-only capacity (the Qwen ViT is frozen in the base recipe, ours `GF1_FUSION_CONNECTOR_20261111.md`), and our early LM layers are the first place where tokens of different frames meet; vision-only branches there may let the model compute cross-frame pose that the per-frame ViT cannot give (ours: ViT tokens probe at chance, LM layer 16 halves the error).
3. It does not argue for removing the G3T tower, for swapping the Qwen ViT, or for more frames; the tower gives 3 deg pose (ours) from far more 3D training than our ~200K pose rows can teach new modules.
4. First experiment IF-P0: the posediag frozen-feature probe protocol with zero-initialized in-fusion branches added to layers 1-8 of frozen T0 m800; about 3-6 H100-h per arm (E); read held-out direction error at m300.
5. Build the IF-P0 kit now (CPU-testable, off the LEG-S1 critical path) and run it on the first idle card after LEG-S1's step-100 decision; integrate in-fusion into a full-FT leg only if IF-P0 passes.

## What the paper shows (all numbers paper)
- **Setup.** A Qwen3-style dense decoder (1.7B and 4B main; ladder 0.6B-6.4B), all recipes trained from scratch on common data (Nemotron-CC text, MetaCLIP 1.2 pairs recaptioned by Qwen2.5-VL-32B, 70:30 text to image-text) at matched lifetime compute (6.5e20 to 2.9e22 FLOPs; instruction tuning excluded). Encoder: a 27-layer, 0.4B ViT. Images 224 px (256 tokens) in pretraining, 448 px (1,024 tokens) in Cambrian-7M instruction tuning (3 epochs). Avg VQA = mean of four category means (general, knowledge, OCR and chart, vision-centric) over 16 Cambrian benchmarks; Avg Text over 12 benchmarks. One peak learning rate per size for every recipe (3e-4 at 1.7B, 2e-4 at 4B); no separate rate for the new modules is stated.
- **Prior optional.** An encoder trained jointly from scratch is on par with one pretrained on the same data; spending 60% of the budget on encoder pretraining hurts. A free MetaCLIP encoder trails the scratch encoder (by 1.8 at the largest 4B budget) and, in midtraining, trails "mostly on OCR, chart, and vision-centric questions".
- **Capacity matters.** At 1.7B and 2.6e21 FLOPs: scratch encoder 44.4 Avg VQA, encoder-free 39.0; making the encoder-free backbone wider or deeper gives 38.6-38.8. Encoder-free trails by 4.1-6.4 at 1.7B and 2.8-6.8 at 4B, and needs 2.6-3.1x the encoder's compute to match it (in-fusion 1.0-1.1x; fit RMS 0.93 points). The gap is largest on OCR and charts.
- **Placement (Table 3, 1.7B, +0.4B, FFN-only branches).** Layers 1-8: 45.0 (OCR 36.0); every layer (narrow): 39.4; spread 8 of 28: 37.6; layers 21-28: 35.1; all-token shared FFN widening in 1-8: 40.1; all-token separate branch in 1-8: 42.3. Routing to vision tokens also wins at 5.2e21 (46.7 against 45.4).
- **Note on the headline.** The 45.0 against 44.4 is the FFN-only variant. The default variant (FFN branch plus vision-only Q/K/V/O deltas) scores 42.6 at that budget and 49.2 against the encoder's 48.9 and FFN-only's 48.6 at 1.3e22; at 4B, 2.9e22: encoder 54.5, in-fusion 54.3, FFN-only 53.4, encoder-free 51.7. In-fusion minus a same-width all-token branch at 4B: +1.1 [0.4, 1.9]. The variants differ by about 1-2 points; the encoder-free gap (3-7) is the robust effect.
- **Midtraining from a pretrained 1.7B text model** (1T then to 1.33T token-equivalents, project page): in-fusion 50.1, scratch encoder 48.6, encoder-free 39.8, MoT (mixture of transformers: separate vision weights) on layers 1-8 48.5, full MoT 43.3 with 3.5x the vision parameters. Avg Text stays within a point of a text-only control for every recipe. Whether any weight was frozen here is not stated in the text read.
- **Speed** (project page; one H200, 16 concurrent requests, one 448 px image, 256 text tokens): TTFT 37 ms in-fusion, 64 ms encoder, 100 ms full MoT; training speed equal to encoder-free, encoder 3% slower at 4B.
- **Not covered**: video, multiple images, interleaved data ("remains open"), higher resolution, sparse MoE, pretrained VLMs, spatial or geometric tasks, and released encoders trained on more data ("our results do not rule out a benefit").

## Mechanism (paper, section 5.1 and Appendix E.1)
- In each augmented layer every token still passes the shared weights. For P in {Q, K, V, O}: P(z_i) = W_P z_i + m_i dW_P z_i, with m_i = 1 for vision tokens, 0 otherwise. The FFN gains a vision-only SwiGLU branch on the same normalized input: h' = h + F(N(h)) + m * dF(N(h)). Attention stays one joint pass over text and vision; vision tokens attend bidirectionally within the image, text causally.
- dW_P and the output projection of dF are zero-initialized, so training starts from the shared model and text-only inputs are computed exactly as before.
- Cost: an FFN branch adds L_a(3DF + D) parameters. At 1.7B the default uses width 6,144 (+302.0M FFN, +100.7M QKVO); at 4B width 3,136 (+192.7M, +209.7M). Extra compute applies only to vision tokens.
- Why early: the authors "hypothesize that vision tokens benefit most from vision-only processing while they are still close to raw patch features"; layer similarity shows in-fusion models change vision tokens mostly in blocks 1-8, while encoder-free models keep changing them through shared blocks 4-13. Spread or late placement dilutes the budget.
- For our LM (E arithmetic, width 4096 and 32 layers from the GF-1 record, about 130 visual tokens per frame): a width-4096 branch adds about 50M parameters per layer, about 0.40B over layers 1-8 (4.5% of 9B), and about 3.4 TFLOP per 32-frame forward, roughly 4% of the LM forward.
