# K1 ground-truth rasters: clean-room rebuild (2026-11-15)

**Question.** The 300 K1 ground-truth rasters and their builder were lost with trinity-2-18. Can they be rebuilt so each file equals its sha256 pin in kit_k1a CAPTURES.json?

**Answer.** Yes, for every rule tested. A clean-room builder reproduces six pinned rasters byte for byte (ScanNet scene0475_01, ScanNet++ 17c5e6a336, ARKit 42899922, 41069053, 41098139, 45261332), including two ARKit captures held out from the one fitted constant. All 300 captures' inputs exist on /data2 (r1313 assets via stagec_gen SCENE.json) and /data3 (ARKitScenes 3dod). The bulk costs about 3.64 GB of governed reads, about 1 CPU-hour, and 1.37 GB of output.

**Evidence.** orch_n30/events/gt_rasters/GT_RASTER_REBUILD_SCOPE.md (rules, proof table, price); builder and per-capture inputs in GT_RASTER_BUILDER_CR.tar.gz in the same folder (sha256 381944a5338a2c63e225e8fa3b39b597f95b47945e056edf185c0dfab568d34e); lane folder /scratch/jjyeung/orch_n30/gt_rasters on trinity-1-18.

**Options rejected.** Waiting for trinity-2-18: no date, and the rebuild needs under an hour of CPU. Rebuilding with a tolerance instead of pins: unnecessary, since the arrays match exactly. Inferring ARKit rotation from metadata sky_direction: 3 of 26 local "Left" captures are landscape; the pose-gravity rule fits all 52.

**What might not work, and how we notice.** Three ARKit pilot captures have a gap in their 10 Hz timeline (41125837, 42899811, 42899906), and one rotates counterclockwise (47204482). Their index-to-frame mapping and rotation sign are untested. A sha256 mismatch against the pin flags each failure, and the capture is deferred, never substituted.

**Tests that would settle it.** Rebuild the 79 local pinned rasters on 1-18 as a regression, then the 300, and sha256 each file against CAPTURES.json.

**Status.** 296 of 300 rasters equal their pins (ScanNet 51/51, ScanNet++ 86/87, ARKit 159/162). Lane gt_build rebuilt 259 on trinity-1-18; lane gt_build2 recovered 37 more ARKit with one rule set R1, which also reproduces all 122 earlier ARKit matches. R1 extends the clean-room ARKit builder: the 180-degree rule for upside-down landscape captures; a box whose axis-aligned bottom is at or above the floor cut keeps its points below the cut; the cut removes only points within 5 cm of the 2nd-percentile z, in either direction; a frame is missing when its pose is more than 500 microseconds away after rounding; and points are lifted from float32 depth with the float64 pose. The exemption and band rules come from relifting the 59 local original arkit_gt files from their own arrays (57 reproduce exactly). Every file is accepted only on sha256 equality. Unresolved: ARKit 41125829, 42899325 and 45261532 (inst invariant under every box rule tried) and ScanNet++ ef69d58016 (17 bytes short; ray ties at face edges excluded).
