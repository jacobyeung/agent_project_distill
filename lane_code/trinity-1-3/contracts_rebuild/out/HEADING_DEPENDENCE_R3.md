# Heading dependence

The public level frame uses y for gravity-up and x/z for horizontal coordinates. World geometry uses z for gravity-up. The table covers every printed field in the decode, shared formatter, calculator, and keypoint paths. Box axes and metric operands remain private and are not additional printed fields.

A no entry means no heading gate; the math column distinguishes geometric independence from calculator operand arithmetic. Quantization, coordinate-wise medians, box-yaw rounding, and room-grid rasterization are retained; they are not mathematically rotation-equivariant at all precision boundaries. Byte-parity tests hold the scene and its numeric representation fixed and change only heading availability. No test claims arbitrary re-rotation preserves every rounded digit.

Tests are in `work/tests_r3/test_heading_r2.py`, with inherited fixture checks in `test_contracts.py` and `test_edges.py`. Results and executed lines are recorded in `out/FINAL_TEST_RESULTS_R3.json` and `out/COVERAGE_DETAIL_R3.json`.
