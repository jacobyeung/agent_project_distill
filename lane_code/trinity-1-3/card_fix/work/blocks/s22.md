### 2.2 The registration: question, difference, confounds, rule, decision
1. **Question.** After SFT on GT traces that add recovery, unit and traced room rows and the missing direct families, does one checkpoint place its calls, terminate, and answer from the responses when every call is served inside the checkpoint, without losing direct types?
2. **The one difference from its comparison.** The comparison is step 544 read by the same reader build, on the same arm and the same items; the difference is R1's training. The rows are a bundle by the stack-first rule.
3. **Confounds.** Warm start with a fresh optimizer and a restarted schedule (D0's m100 carried a dip). Six row families at once, entering at different boundaries, so a difference at step 400 mixes 400 steps of the launch families with 200 steps of the later ones. D0's size loss has an unverified cause (slot SIZE-V, section 5). The parents of step 544 saw dev-S1's scenes, which favours direct answers there; both arms of every paired comparison share that exposure, and only R0-dev is unexposed (5, item 6). Recovery rows print simulated statuses. G3T and SAM 3 have unaudited pretraining scenes. The depth and gravity heads keep training and set the tower arm's scale and level frame, so a camera or distance change on the tower arm can come from them. Vehicles P and N carry different auxiliary losses (N trains the scale token). If A2 enters at a passing boundary, every later comparison with step 544 includes GF-1.
4. **Pre-registered rule.** One decision is taken per boundary (steps 200, 400, 600, 800), on dev-S1, against step 544. R0-dev is printed beside every number and stops nothing alone. Intervals are percentile intervals of a bootstrap over whole physical rooms, 10,000 draws, with the seed each tool pins (`f1f2.py` 17, the evaluator 20261115). The read tools take the interval level as a parameter; `room_bootstrap.py` prints 95% only today, and cont_ops owes that change before step 200.

   **4a. Harm rule.** The leg stops and exports when H1, H2 or H3 holds.
   - **H1, types.** One of the seven direct types (route planning, counting, direction easy, medium and hard, relative distance, object size; 150 items each) has the interval of its paired difference wholly below zero. The level is 1 - 0.05/7 = 99.29% at step 200 and 1 - 0.05/28 = 99.82% at steps 400, 600 and 800.
   - **H2, mean.** The interval of the unweighted mean of the seven paired differences lies wholly below zero, with rooms resampled jointly over the 72 rooms that hold the 1,050 items. The level is 95% at step 200 and 98.75% later.
   - **H3, camera on the tower arm.** One of the three camera types (150 items each) has its interval, at H1's level, wholly below zero, or fewer than 95% of the 450 camera items draw a call (the call floor of the trace ruling's F3; step 544 drew a call on 1,050 of 1,050 camera reads, job 170631). H3 applies only when step 544's tower-arm read on the same reader build exists (section 4, rank 2); until then the camera types are printed and cannot stop the leg.

   The noise model is the D0 m200 read against step 544 (job 170320; `F200.json`, `A544.jsonl` and `ITEMS.jsonl` under `C/d0/readk/`): 1,050 paired items in 72 rooms, 95% half-widths of 4.3 to 7.9 points per type and 2.4 on the mean of seven, and correlations between types from -0.42 to +0.37 (mean +0.02), so the tests are close to independent. "No change" resamples whole rooms and exchanges the two arms inside each drawn room with probability 1/2; "flips" sets each item of the later arm to zero with probability loss / mean score. Each of 8,000 pseudo-reads is scored with the tool's own interval, 10,000 inner draws (Monte Carlo error at most 0.006). The percentile interval on 35 to 59 rooms is anti-conservative, which is why the rule stops an unchanged model 6.3% of the time and not 5.0%; 95% intervals on the seven types without the correction would stop it 17.9% of the time. Applied to D0's m200 read, the rule stops on size ([-17.97, -1.44] at the step-200 level) and on nothing else (mean of seven -0.68 [-3.17, +1.60]). Scripts and outputs: `CF/a3/` (`A3_POWER_PASS2.json`, `A3_POWER.json`, `D0_M200_UNDER_R1_RULE.json`), copied to `EV/next_leg/card_fix_a3_evidence.tgz`.

   Over the leg, H1 or H2 stops an unchanged model with probability at most 0.063 + 3 x 0.023 = 0.132, a union bound over the four looks. Two parts are not calibrated. H3's camera intervals have no paired tower-arm item file; by analogy with H1's worst type they add at most 0.024 at step 200 and 0.010 at each later boundary (guess), 0.186 over the leg with H1 and H2. The correlation between looks is unmeasured; D0's m100 item file (job 170786, running) joined with m200 gives it, and it can only lower the leg-wide figure. Each type's test detects half the time a loss equal to its Bonferroni half-width, 5.9 to 10.8 points at step 200, so the rule is a guard against large losses, not a certificate of no loss.

   **4c. Verdict of a boundary.** STOP: the harm rule fires. HOLD: the harm rule is silent and a gate due at this boundary fails or cannot be evaluated; training continues, the rows lane rebuilds that family, and the gate is read again at the next boundary with twice the dose. PASS: the harm rule is silent and every gate due so far has passed. A boundary is a passing boundary when it is a PASS and the recovery and unit gates are among those passed, which makes step 200 the earliest if the launch vehicle carries both families and step 400 otherwise. A family that has not entered is named on the card and claims nothing.
5. **Decision it changes.** PASS: R1 continues. At a passing boundary A2 may enter as a new pin, and the export becomes a possible base. At step 1,000 the export the init rule's tests select gets the leg's one official read, through a reader that passes EAT-1, and becomes the base for GF-1 and RL; that read is reported and decides nothing. STOP on size alone: size moves to a head route (H1-2, size first) before any further step, and the size rows leave the store. STOP on another type or on H2: the family that feeds the failing type is rebuilt, and the leg restarts from its last passing export, or from step 544 if none passed. STOP on H3: the serving pins and the camera rows are audited before any restart. A gate that fails twice in a row stops the leg at that boundary; it restarts from the last passing export with the rebuilt family.
