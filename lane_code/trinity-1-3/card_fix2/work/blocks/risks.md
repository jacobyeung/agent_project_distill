## What might not work, and how we would notice
- **Object size drifts although no size row trains.** Lane size_diag expects 0 to -6 points on dev-S1 (guess), and H1 stops the leg on a drift of -6 with estimated probability 0.17 to 0.21 at step 200. Sign: object size at m100 and m200 against step 544, split by metric and imperial items. A stop on size ends the leg under this card; the new card decides whether size moves to a head route.
- **The sealed tree refuses families,** so P carries part of R1: all recovery rows fail its `not_seen` membership rule, and unit rows pass its two-decimal label test only in the faithful slice (`RP/ROUTING_DIAGNOSIS.json`). Sign: the loader audit's counts per family and per unit. Recovery enters when N takes over, and a unit with fewer than 100 eligible rows is named missing.
- **The faithful unit slice teaches feet almost alone.** It holds 1,224 of the 10,945 rows: feet 1,130, inches 94, centimetres 0. The unit gate counts all 107 dev items, 61 of them in inches or centimetres, so on P it needs 6 to 14 more converted items (2.2, item 4b), which the 46 feet items must supply unless the conversion line carries over to the other units. Sign: the unit gate at step 200, printed per unit; the remedy is vehicle N's full unit family. The slice's 710 ScanNet++ rows also wait for a box-extent audit.
- **Capped reads exceed 5 of 150 on the claimed arm without recovery rows.** About 7 of 150 reads draw a status under the best-candidate selector, and half of the own arm's status reads end at the cap. Sign: the capped count of the tower-arm baseline. If the baseline already exceeds 5, the requirement cannot hold without recovery rows, and the orchestrator rules before the first export is read.
- **Recovery rows teach a direct estimate the lineage never trained** (no direct distance rows since the sealed leg), so answers after `not seen` may be poor. Sign: MRA on recovered reads against the constant; the lever is then grounding recall, not rows.
- **The room call survives, or room answers do not improve.** The traced rows name landmarks and then state the area; they do not derive it, so little gain beyond answer supervision is expected (v7's review, finding 1). Sign: the room gate's MRA and its call-rate bound, 200 steps after the traced rows enter.
- **The facing gate passes on a shortcut.** "Away means back" answers 84.5% of the away-hard training rows without reading the forward relation. Sign: away accuracy by forward band, and toward-facing accuracy, a protected outcome.
- **The harm rule misses a real loss.** Against a loss of D0's size on one type it stops the leg with estimated probability 0.40 to 0.72 at step 200. Sign: the printed intervals at every boundary; the init rule's tests on the final export catch what the boundaries miss.
- **A baseline is missing when its gate falls due.** The gate is then HOLD, and a VSTI or facing gate that has not passed at step 400 ends the leg. Sign: the registration list of 2.2 item 4d, checked when the step-200 export lands.
- **The type clause of the VSTI gate holds a working repair.** If 5% of right answers flip back on a type with a 94% baseline, that type loses ground, and the gate then needs about +8 pooled points where the pooled test alone needs +3 (planning value, assumed baseline). Sign: the per-type baselines of the VSTI dev read, known before any export is read.
- **A gate passes on the wrong arm.** The registered baselines are read on the claimed arm with the pinned selector; the own-arm numbers on the card are planning values. Sign: a boundary report that prints a gate without its registered baseline's sha256.
- **The recovery dose is too small.** 200 draws by the gate may not teach the continuation. Sign: the recovery gate is a HOLD; the cell then rises to 2.00.
- **The fixed-math scale fails EAT-1's A3 scan** even with its constants as buffers. Sign: rank 3; fallback: the residual head, or the pose head's scale token if it prices within the rule.
- **The slot opens early and no proof has passed.** Then the four H100 wait; nothing else here is worth 4 H100 unproven. Sign: the launch gates (3.3, item 9).
