## Revised VLA design

All 26 review findings were accepted. The main changes:

- **The "where" provider now fits our code.**
  - A new adapter runs the geometry tower on five frames: the current wrist and agent views plus three earlier wrist frames.
  - The student's own yes/no presence and pointing answers pick among SAM 3 masks.
  - Depth comes from the tower.
  - Metric scale comes from the robot's own motion plus a fitted camera-to-gripper transform.
- **Two claims, each with one primary endpoint:**
  - **Manipulation:** student with "where" against student without, on LIBERO-Spatial at 5 demos per task, over 500 paired episodes. That resolves about ±3 to ±5.5 points.
  - **Navigation:** waypoint clicking against text actions on R2R-CE at 10% of the data, over 600 val-unseen episodes. That resolves about ±2.5 to ±5 points.
  - Each has an explicit "inconclusive" outcome.
- **Attribution without training:** phrase-only, shuffled-geometry and wrong-scale checks separate spatial understanding from the other things the unique arm adds.
- **Embodiments:** the record now covers π0, π0.5 and six other VLAs. All fine-tune per robot body, so our one-head-per-body tests are standard practice.
- **One schema for all lanes,** with six worked numerical examples and eleven conformance tests.
