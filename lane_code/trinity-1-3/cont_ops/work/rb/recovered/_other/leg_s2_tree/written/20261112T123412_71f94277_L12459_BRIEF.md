## Why
Our student is a 9B video-language model with a geometry tower. It answers spatial questions either directly or through a short head-backed program. The training leg about to launch (LEG-S1) uses a frozen route table and no flag: program rows only for five types, direct rows for all others. Three things were moved to the next leg, and none needs a GPU to build and test:
1. Extra row sets that repair measured supervision holes: "facing-away" direction rows (the student answers them rotated by 180 degrees: 14.0 against 91.3 on forward twins) and multi-category "total count" rows (the student lists separate counts and never sums).
2. A per-row form flag, so one checkpoint can be read forced-direct and forced-program on the same items, and so the opposite-form rows (about 76,000 program rows of direct-by-default types, deferred in LEG-S1) can train without teaching the unflagged policy the wrong path.
3. A first-batch dry-run of a candidate next-leg mix through the full forward pass on CPU.

## Part 2: the per-row form flag
Design it in out/FLAG_DESIGN.md first (one page), then implement it in work/tree_s2 on top of Part 1.
- Two fixed prompt strings, one forcing the direct form and one forcing the program form, plus no flag. A row's flag is a per-row field decided at preparation by a deterministic hash, never one receipt-wide value. Rows whose form agrees with the route table's default for their type are flagged with a configurable share (default 1/2) and unflagged otherwise. Rows of the opposite form are always flagged. An unflagged row therefore always shows the table's path.
- The flag text is added to the prompt in exactly one place for training and evaluation; direct rows can carry it; the receipt binds the two strings, the share, the seed and the route file's sha256, and resume refuses a mismatch.
- Sampling: deterministic, with the flagged and unflagged counts per type and form reported by preparation and bound in the receipt. No change to stream sizes unless the config says so.
- Evaluation (the tree's development read path, evalpath/run.py): three modes per read (no flag, force direct, force program), chosen by the operator for the whole read, never per question type; forced reads are marked ineligible for headline numbers, as today.
- Gates: the LEG-S1 builder, launch gate and in-job admission refuse any flag. Keep that behaviour for a config that names the S1 profile, and add an S2 profile that requires the full flag contract (both strings, share, seed) and refuses a half-configured one.
- Tests, each failing when its change is reverted: an unflagged row never has the opposite form; the flagged share per type is within one row of the configured share; the flag text never appears in an unflagged row's token ids; resume with a changed string, share, seed or route file refuses; evaluation's three modes produce the three prompts; with the flag contract absent the tree is byte-identical to Part 1 on the fixtures; the measure R1b of the plan (the unflagged path taken per type, and unflagged minus forced score) can be computed by a report tool from three evaluation journals (build the tool, test it on synthetic journals).
Deliver out/TREE_S2B seal, out/FLAG_DESIGN.md, out/PART2_TESTS.md.
