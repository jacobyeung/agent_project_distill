# Room-call pricing specification: Part A

The executable protocol is OPERATOR.md. The immutable source specification is evidence/KIT_SPEC_room_pricing.original.md; the review is review/REVIEW_VERDICT.md. The following contract governs this kit.

1. Freeze R1 step 200, read 0, journal 4/0, baseline SHA256 c9b5994cc461420a0ccfb035cd252e27bac6da3016ab5d5a28f77a14239b319d before R0-dev inference. Use unrounded D+5. Any replacement requires a new pre-inference registration.
2. PASS/FAIL evaluates this complete measurement pipeline against R0 mesh labels, not geometry quality alone. Both arms keep the original labels. A sensor-depth label audit is diagnostic for every outcome and cannot relabel, calibrate or reopen these rooms.
3. R0-dev is excluded from student training but already observed in direct-answer diagnostics; those diagnostics cannot adjust this candidate. K1 held-out, R0-dev and benchmark rooms fit no rule, threshold, frame budget, admission policy or checkpoint. This exclusion also governs the incorporated training-row plan. T10 and u94 are report-only; adaptive reruns are forbidden.
4. Require hashed evidence of this kernel's relation to the benchmark-selected rule and authorized K1-train-only selection. If benchmark outcomes selected it, do not certify a clean held-out design. Unexposed describes only the audited student-training boundary. Tower pretraining overlap is unknown.
5. Full local digests and pinned frame manifests govern S0 and resume. Remote read/checkpoint/frame receipts remain MISSING until authenticated source records arrive. A direct export without frame indices does not prove reader equality for either evaluation set.
6. The lane-local adapter calls the unchanged deployed kernel, extracts only the harness's pure functions, and unpacks its R0 tuple. It maps depth_grid64/anchor_slots to depth_grid/item_slots. Cache and TMPDIR paths stay inside the lane. S1 requires actual CPU forwards; S1b accepts the equivalent local stored-prediction reproduction.
7. Absent, valid and corrupt-or-stale receipts are distinct. Skip only exact prediction/frame/slot/model/weight/code/setting matches; stop on corruption or mismatch. GO binds host, physical card and run. Verify exactly one actual visible CUDA device. Never automatically rerun a cluster-killed job.
8. DA3 f32+R0 is the sole confirmatory adoption test. PASS requires an observed gain of at least five and paired 95% lower bound above zero. Part B and every other arm are exploratory. A confirmatory trained-route claim requires a fresh unexposed set. Missing primary/items, duplicate baseline IDs and invalid cached scores are fatal.
9. No power probabilities are asserted. The supplied evidence does not contain their simulation.
10. The expected 0.6 GPU-hours is an estimate. Enforce 1.5 GPU-hours across loads, captures and retries. Measure the first u94 capture's peak memory before the full secondary pass; OOM drops that arm at unchanged resolution. Itemize the optional guard and Part B separately.

Inputs, exact CPU/GPU commands, GO fields, output schema and unresolved paths are in OPERATOR.md. Full source hashes are in configs/SNAPSHOT_INPUTS.json, review/LOCAL_INPUT_SHA256.txt and out/S0.json. The fixed DA3 protocol is 504 px, saddle_balanced, seed 0, bf16 on CUDA, native metric scale 1.
