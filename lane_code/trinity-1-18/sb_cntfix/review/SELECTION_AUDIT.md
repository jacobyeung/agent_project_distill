Selection audit of the supplied kit

The binding rule requires every rule and threshold to come from K1 training captures. R0-dev permits one preregistered comparison per category; dev-S1 is diagnostic. The supplied spec does not implement that protocol.

| Selection point | Items used or exposed | Finding and required edit |
|---|---|---|
| Per-frame maximum versus second maximum, joint threshold, duplicate suppression | `k1_floor.py` sweeps raw/NMS, max/second, thresholds 0.35–0.90; reports all 2,435 pairs and train/held-out large subsets (196/58). | Spec §4 selects the best NMS rule on training large sets but admits held-out visibility. `K1_FLOOR.json` gives raw maximum 44.8 training versus selected NMS 41.7; held-out scores are 37.4 versus 45.2. This does not prove held-out selection, but the NMS restriction has no training-only selection receipt. Freeze the candidate family and selection objective using training captures only. |
