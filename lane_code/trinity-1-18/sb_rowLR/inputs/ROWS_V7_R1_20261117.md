## What might not work, and how we would notice
- Recovery policy conflict: single first-operand misses stop without grounding the second object, while two-miss rows ground it after the first miss. Reads after R1 would show either policy after a first `not seen`; count both in the m200 traces.
- The native GT boxes behind the recovery, unit and absolute-distance rows are half extents on every `scannetpp/` (v1 asset) scene, measured per box by depth containment against GT rasters (`L/out/extent_audit/REPORT.md`): those rows print half-size boxes and their distance labels are too large (corrected/stored median 0.86). Half boxes appear in 679 of 1,600 recovery v2 rows, 361 of 1,224 rows of the unit slice for vehicle P, and 593, 435 and 624 rows of build 9's bound distance, recovery and unit slices. Rows whose boxes stay unknown are left out of every repaired family; unknown does not mean half.
- Room traces add landmarks the model can name but give no measured cue; room accuracy may not move. The m200 read of room MRA against step 544 shows it.
- v6 holds 6,159 rows on 92 K1 held-out rooms; a K1 held-out evaluation of anything the student trains on would be contaminated.
- The away wording trains ReVSI's question phrase as a template; it needs the legality review the rows-lane rule names.

## Tests that would settle it
