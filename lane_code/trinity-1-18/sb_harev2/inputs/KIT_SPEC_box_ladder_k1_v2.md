## 4. Fixed math: the object set (a new rule, chosen on train; it is not the linker's rule)
The linker (`routed_candidates.py` lines 141 to 149) links two proposals of different frames when their centres lie within `max(0.30, 0.5 * min extent) + 0.05 * mean depth` metres or when `overlap = |A ∩ B| / max(1, min(|A|, |B|))` of their 10 cm voxel sets is at least 0.25; it then peels one proposal per frame, which splits one object into several candidates.
- **Input:** the accepted candidates that `StageOne.propose(name)` returns for one name, in its order (index 0, 1, ...). Each holds at most one proposal per frame. `V(C)` = `Candidate.voxels`.
- **Overlap:** `ov(A, B) = routed_candidates.overlap(V(A), V(B))`, so the denominator is the smaller voxel set. The centre-distance condition is not used: neighbouring instances of one name have close centres.
- **Same-frame rule:** in a frame where both objects hold a mask, `contain = |mA ∧ mB| / max(1, min(|mA|, |mB|))` on the 80 x 112 raster. Two objects may merge only if `contain >= 0.5` in every frame they share; two masks of one frame that do not overlap are two instances.
- **Merge order:** every candidate starts as an object. Repeat until no pair qualifies: among the pairs that may merge and have `ov >= tau`, merge the pair with the largest `ov` (ties: the pair with the lowest smaller index, then the lowest larger index). The merged object keeps, in a shared frame, the member with the larger mask area (ties: higher `joint`, then lower proposal `index`), takes the lower index, and its voxels are recomputed. `tau` is one of 0.15, 0.25 (the linker's constant), 0.40.
- **Printed for a single-object call:** the object that contains the selector's choice. Frame list = its member frames, ascending; first frame = the lowest; best frame = the member with the largest mask area.
- **Count (diagnostic, section 6):** `n_all` = the number of objects of the name; `n_conf` = the number with a member whose `score` is at least 0.5. Neither constant is tuned on any set.
- **Fusion rules (11).** Per-frame points are that frame's `bestspread` points (the cut of `lift_bestspread` applied in each member frame). `best1` = the best frame's points (today's rule on the object); `vote_<v>_<m>` = the points in voxels of edge `v` (5 or 10 cm) that at least `m` (2 or 3) member frames support, and `vote_<v>_<m>_best` = those united with the best frame's points (a vote rule equals `best1` when the object has fewer than `m` member frames); `allspread` = every member frame's points; `trim` = `allspread` with the box extents cut to the 2nd and 98th percentile along each box axis. Box = `min_area_box`. With one member frame every rule equals `best1`.
- **Choices that trace to an official test set, re-derived here (ruling 06:07Z).** The fusion policy of 2026-11-11 (cut each mask to its interior depth, answer distance from the largest single view, fit other boxes to points that two frames agree on in 5 cm voxels plus the largest view) was first found on benchmark test scenes. (a) Its voted box is the `vote` family: this kit re-derives the voxel edge, the support and the use of the best view on K1 train, by the cross-fit of section 7, against rules that owe nothing to that policy. (b) Its single largest view with a depth cut is today's `lift_bestspread`, which was registered and priced on K1 train on 2026-11-16 (`E2E_READER_B04_20261116.md`); it enters the same cross-fit as `best1`. Nothing else in this kit comes from that policy.

## 6. Diagnostics for every arm (no extra pass)
- **Size.** Objects = the distinct (capture, GT instance) operands of the train rows. GT full extents: `K1_ROW_AUDIT.jsonl` (same folder as the labels) gives each row's two `box_keys` in operand order; `K1_BOX_AUDIT.json` (f02a8dc8...) gives `box.axesLengths`; the row's `convention` in the label file decides: `full` uses them as stored, `half` doubles them (on all 1,253 registered rows `corrected` equals `x1_m` for `full` and `x2_m` for `half`, and no capture mixes conventions; checked). Report the printed longest edge over the GT longest edge and its MRA, per arm.
- **First seen.** GT first frame of an instance = the first anchor whose GT raster share is at least 0.002. Over the pairs of distinct operand instances of one capture whose GT first frames differ: the share of pairs whose printed first frames stand in the true order; a `not seen` operand makes its pairs wrong; equal printed frames make the pair wrong. Exact and within-one rates are reported and decide nothing.
- **Members.** A member added by a merge is correct when the GT instance covering most of its mask equals the GT instance covering most of the seed candidate's masks. Report added-member precision, frames before and after, and per (capture, operand name): `n_all`, `n_conf` and the number of objects whose majority instance is the operand's. Every K1 operand name maps to one GT instance (975 of 975 in the manifest), so K1 measures splits of one object and cannot measure merges of two instances.
- Gaps by truth band (under 1 m, 1 to 2, 2 to 4, above 4), by dataset, and by minimum member frames.

## 7. Statistics
MRA x 100 with the harness scorer (`agg.py` f2b99544...), unchanged; item-weighted means; 95% percentile bootstrap over physical rooms, 10,000 draws, seed 20261119, paired on the contrast's row set.
**Cross-fitted selection** (so that train both selects and tests): sort the 192 train rooms by id, permute with `numpy.random.default_rng(20261119)`, fold = position mod 5. For fold k, among the 33 variants `tau x rule` whose added-member precision on the other four folds is at least 0.90, take the one with the highest mean MRA on the other four folds (ties: `tau` 0.25, 0.15, 0.40, then `best1`, `trim`, `allspread`, then the `vote` rules with the best view and without it, each in the order 5 cm before 10 cm and support 2 before 3); if none qualifies the fold predicts with A0. `obj_cf` = each row predicted by its fold's variant. The frozen variant is the same choice made on all train rooms. The report also prints every variant's in-sample train MRA, labelled in-sample.

## 8. CPU dry run (train captures only; about 10 minutes)
Two train captures (arkitscenes/41048124, scannetpp/036bce3393), all arms. Required:
(a) **stored-label parity:** the kit's `S4+G1+bestspread` GT-mask predictions equal the stored `b04host3/lift_price/harness/out_lift_train_v2` rows of those captures, scored with stored labels;
(b) **corrected-label parity:** scoring the stored predictions of `k1_ops3/work/e3gd/out/PER_ITEM.jsonl` (a4b69330...) on the train set with the corrected labels gives 74.30 for arm `GD_reviewed` and 93.07 for arm `GT` (the review's recomputation, `sb_harev/out/RECOMPUTE.json`, rule `relative error < 1 - theta`), to 0.01; if the harness scorer and that rule differ on a row, list the rows and keep the harness scorer. Corrected-label rows are never required to equal stored-label rows;
(c) on the objects of those two captures and of five more train captures of the table set, A0's stage-1 replay under rule `null_argmax` picks the candidate `run_iso` picks in the labelled tables for at least 95% of objects (a lower rate is a BLOCKER);
(d) every fusion rule equals `best1` on a one-frame object, and `tau = 1.01` (no merge) makes A4 `best1` equal A0;
(e) `gtgeo_fused` runs. These checks prove that the code matches stored outputs; they say nothing about whether a rule helps.
Then the train pass in four shards (the `[k n]` arguments of `lift_price.py`), about 6 to 8 CPU-core-hours.

## 9. Bars, written before any number (train only)
- **`replace_box`, in two parts, both on train:** (1) this kit: `obj_cf` - A0 on the 1,037 train rows is at least +3.0 type points with a paired 95% lower bound above 0; (2) kit `tower_frames` Part A: the frozen variant minus A0, both rebuilt at 32 input frames, has a paired lower bound above 0. The frozen object variant replaces today's box only when both hold; otherwise today's rule stays. `DECISION.json` here records part 1.
- **`fused_path` (attribution only):** the cross-fitted best of the ten fused rules on GT masks against A2: at least +3.0 with a lower bound above 0 reads "one view costs at least 3 points under this tower"; under +1.0 reads "the single view is not the limit under this tower".
- **`merge_safe`:** added-member precision of the frozen variant at least 0.90 on train.
- **`selector_worth`:** A1 - A0 at least +4.0 on the table set. **`selector_adopt`:** the out-of-fold A8 minus A0 on the table set with a lower bound above 0.
- **`size_screen`:** the size MRA of `obj_cf` boxes minus that of A0 boxes, paired by object, at least +5.0 with a lower bound above 0. A pass sends size to its one look in kit `object_route_r0dev`; a fail keeps size direct and that kit does not run size.
- **`first_seen_screen`:** at least 0.90 of train operand pairs ordered correctly by the printed first frames, for source `linker` (A0's frames) and source `object` (the frozen variant's frames) separately. Kit `object_route_r0dev` adds source `track`. The source with the highest rate among those at or above 0.90 goes to the appearance-order look; with none, appearance order is not run.
- **`tower_challenger` (report):** DA3 or Pi3X under the frozen fusion rule against GD under it, on the 1,037 rows with refusals at 0.
