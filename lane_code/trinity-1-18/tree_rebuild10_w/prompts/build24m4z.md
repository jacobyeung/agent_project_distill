The Orchard project filesystem (`/project/community`, where `X=/project/community/jjyeung/okh7/x_22c714e1` lives) is at about 41 GiB free and another tenant writes up to 18 GiB a minute. A 24-hour job must survive that filesystem returning "no space left". Before the launch question we must know, by a targeted test on the final kit's bytes, what each of these does on ENOSPC:
1. the live-evidence publication every 600 s (and from the EXIT/TERM/USR1 trap) into `X/o/<job>/live/`;
2. the job's own stdout and stderr (the wrapper `jn.sh` sends Slurm `--output/--error` to `X/j/`; `job.sh` pipes `x.py` through `l.py`);
3. receipts the job writes before training starts (v.json, the host receipt h0.json and census c0..c4.json, rv.py's review wait on `X/n_r/review.json`);
4. checkpoints, the stop checkpoint and exports (they must reach GCS without any byte on the project filesystem; read where `b/pc.py` (~line 343) chooses `X/n_ck/<job>/` before GCS);
5. the exit archive.
Acceptable behaviour: training, checkpoints and exports continue; a failed publication is retried and logged on node-local disk; nothing fatal follows from a write failure on the project filesystem after training has started.
