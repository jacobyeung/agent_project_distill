## Why
The free-form v1 arm (format C) passed 6/16 on pilot and ~10% in bulk, because the model wrote raw coordinates in prose and did arithmetic there, failing number grounding. Codex Astra's design memo (`MEMO2.md` §8) defines a constrained variant, C-N: "the model writes only qualitative spatial reasoning, with no numeric measurements, counts, numeral words or frame indices. A deterministic wrapper inserts the selected frame anchors and the immutable final answer. Only the asked numeric quantity may be exposed, and a deterministic verified numeric sentence supplies it; the model cannot rewrite that sentence or introduce other quantities. On multiple-choice questions, its generated prose contains no numbers at all." This isolates C's likely advantage (natural connections between visible entities) from the observed coordinate-arithmetic failure mechanism. §4 sets voice: "Use neutral voice. Write 'The table is visible in frame 6,' not 'I measured' or 'I observe clearly.'" and frame scope: "frame references must resolve to [the student interface's] 1-based positions, never original video indices or hidden teacher views." §3 sets the answer line: "The final line carries only the accepted number, without units, punctuation or an approximation marker. Preserve its bytes." §2 supplies the appearance scope caveat verbatim: "Scan the frames in time order and check each requested category." / "Recorded visibility does not establish absence in other frames or the first appearance in the full video."

## Deliverables
(a) Prompt, versioned `FREEFORM_PROMPT_CN_V1`, sha256 recorded in every target's manifest row and `TRAIN_PARAMS.env`. Content: neutral declarative voice, no first person, no "I observe"; no numeric measurements, counts, numeral words (one, two, first, second, ... — fixed list in module), frame indices, coordinates or units; may name objects, relations (nearer, farther, left, right, in front, behind, above, below, between) and viewpoint changes. Length ceiling 250 words. Plain text, no JSON/markdown.
(b) Deterministic wrapper (not the model) assembles the final target in this order: (1) frame-anchor lines in the student's 1-based delivered positions for the objects the question names, e.g. "The table is visible in frame 6." (2) the model's qualitative passage (3) for numeric questions only, one deterministic verified numeric sentence from a typed calculation/measurement record with units and the record's own precision (SPEC_E §5 display rules: meters two decimals, centimeters integer, no `raw_result` promotion), or omit if no record supports it (4) the accepted answer, byte-exact, last line, no units.
(c) Gates, deterministic, per target, no repair/re-ask: answer line byte-equal; passage contains no digits, no numeral words (fixed list), no unit tokens, no coordinate-like patterns, no frame references (regex list in module) — reject `numeric_leak`; artifact lint as v1; length ceiling 250 words; every object name in the passage must appear among the trace's record identities or the question text, else reject `unknown_entity`; reject "first appears"/"earliest"-type claims unless the wrapper's own recorded-frame sentence supplies them — reject `appearance_overreach`. Tier-II (recorded, not gating): word count, number of relations named, caveat-scope overlap, as v1.
(d) Sidecar per target binding every wrapper line (frame-anchor, numeric sentence, answer) to record ids per the SPEC_E-style fields above, and the passage to `FREEFORM_PROMPT_CN_V1`'s sha256 and the response sha256.
