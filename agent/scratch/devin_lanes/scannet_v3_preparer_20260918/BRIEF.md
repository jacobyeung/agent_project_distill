## Changes
1. Copy the v2 package byte for byte into `preparers/scannet_v3/` in this repository (new directory), keeping its layout, then change only what follows and bump its version and package name to `r1313_vsi_distill_gt_training_scannet_v3`, with a CHANGES.md describing the two changes and their evidence.
2. Non-finite pose at a selected slot: instead of refusing the scene, interpolate the pose from the nearest finite neighbors within 5 frames on each side (translation linear, rotation by slerp on the two neighbors' rotations, or copy the single neighbor when only one side is finite within range), verify the result with the existing `rigid()` check, and record in the scene receipt `interpolated_pose_slots` (slot index, raw frame index, neighbor frame indices used, frame gap). Refuse the scene as before when no finite neighbor exists within 5 frames. The RGB frame at the slot stays the VSI video frame; only the pose is interpolated.
3. Correlation shortfall at a slot: when the raw frame at the slot correlates below 0.99 with the VSI frame, search raw offsets -3..+3 frames, take the offset with the highest correlation, accept only if it reaches 0.99, and record `correlation_offset_slots` (slot, offset, correlation before and after) in the receipt; refuse as before otherwise. The threshold itself does not change.
4. Receipts and the registry entry schema gain only those two optional fields; the verifier accepts them and re-checks that interpolated poses are rigid and that offsets are within range. Everything else in the verifier is unchanged.
5. Tests (`preparers/scannet_v3/tests/`): synthetic pose stream with non-finite slots interpolates and passes `rigid()`; a slot with no finite neighbor within 5 frames refuses; correlation offset search accepts at +2 with recorded fields and refuses when no offset reaches 0.99; a clean stream produces receipts identical in content to v2's fields plus empty new fields.
