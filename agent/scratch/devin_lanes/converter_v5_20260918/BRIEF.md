## Why
Students learn from "detailed explanation + answer" targets converted from the teacher's tool-using SPLIT traces, with every observation, reference frame, relationship, calculation and useful uncertainty or correction kept explicit and traceable to the teacher's evidence. Three converter rounds (v3, v4, v4.1; reports under `agent/scratch/devin_lanes/converter_v{3,4,4_1}_20260918/out/REPORT.md`) built sound semantic structure (fact sheet, identity table, scoped qualifications, semantic self-review, bounded repair) yet produced no candidate, every time because of exact-quote citation plumbing: `citation_not_found` when the model quoted tool-call metadata that the resolver does not index, `citation_ambiguous` when a short quote occurs at several offsets, and offset bookkeeping across UTF-8 normalization. The training purpose needs traceability, not string matching. Citations therefore become identifiers of evidence lines in one canonical rendering that the model sees and the resolver indexes, so a citation is resolvable and unambiguous by construction, and the review effort moves entirely to entailment, scope and identity, where it belongs.

## Required design (orchestrator decisions 2026-09-18 15:25Z)
1. Canonical evidence rendering. Build one deterministic text rendering of the bound teacher archive per question: every evidence entry (tool calls with their arguments, tool returns including warnings and metadata, teacher thoughts and messages, the native final marked as such) rendered as numbered lines `E<entry>.L<line>` with the entry's kind and step. The rendering is what the model receives at every stage and what the resolver indexes; store it with its sha256 beside the candidate. Nothing the model sees is uncitable, and nothing citable is unseen. Lines wrap at a fixed width so line ids are stable; a line id resolves to exactly one string.
2. Citations are sets of line ids (single ids or ranges within one entry). The resolver checks existence and returns the text; there is no quote matching, no offsets, no normalization. A cited native-final line is allowed only in the answer-equality check, never as explanatory evidence (existing rule).
