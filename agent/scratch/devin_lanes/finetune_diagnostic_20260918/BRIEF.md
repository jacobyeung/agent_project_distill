The goal is to show that OneThinker-8B and Qwen3.5-9B fine-tuned with vision and language LoRA on converted teacher targets beat their base checkpoints RGB-only and tool-free on VSIBench, VSTIBench and DSIBench. The trainer (`student_pilot/diagnostic.py`) passed its one-GPU infrastructure smoke for both students (`agent/scratch/devin_lanes/trainer_qwen35_scratch_20260918/out/REPORT.md`, `SMOKE_*.md`): about 20 GiB for OneThinker and 22 GiB for Qwen3.5 at tiny batches. This lane is the first run on real admitted targets; its purpose is to measure whether the pipeline moves held-out accuracy at all and to produce adapters the evaluation harness can score. Scientific rules: the split is scene-disjoint and frozen once (`split.py`, seed 17); no benchmark question, answer, or offline label is ever read by this lane; targets are used exactly as admitted.
