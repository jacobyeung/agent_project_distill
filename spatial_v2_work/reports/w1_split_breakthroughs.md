## 1. What SPLIT Proved Scientifically (Nov 2026)

1. **Perception—not reasoning—is the ~30 pp bottleneck for frontier VLMs, while small models need BOTH perception and thinking (X1, Sampled-Frame GT).**
   - On 450 VSIBench object questions (`X1`), scaling Gemini 3.1 Pro thinking from Low→High on 32-frame video moves accuracy only **+1.4 pp** (`60.2 / 61.8 / 61.6`), whereas supplying GT 3-D scene text jumps **+32 pp** even at Low thinking (`93.7 / 95.0 / 95.9`; Flash video `64.5–68.8` vs GT text Low `94.1`).
   - Blind text-only **Qwen3-8B (thinking)** scores **74.8** (+13.2 over Pro video) and **Qwen3-14B-FP8** scores **76.2** (+14.6). **Critical student caveat:** disabling thinking on Qwen3-8B collapses it to **48.7** (−26.1 pp), and 8B–14B still struggle on absolute distance and route planning. Reasoning is saturated for frontier models, *not* for an 8B–9B student.
   - **No unseen-frame cheat:** Restricting GT strictly to objects visible in the 32 sampled frames keeps nearly the full ceiling—Pro averages **90.6** vs **92.6** whole-scene (VSIB 81.9, VSTI 94.7, ReVSI 95.1); **Flash sampled-frame GT reaches 93.61** (VSIB 88.26, VSTI 93.55, ReVSI 99.04). With GT perception, open-weight Gemma-4-31B (**90.7**) and Qwen3.6-27B (**90.5**) close to within **2.1 pp** of Pro (**92.6**), cutting errors >3×.
2. **Grounding and 3-D geometry fix orthogonal tasks, and most tasks require BOTH (`tab:perception-parts`, `tab:supp-swaps`).**
   - On 493 VSIBench questions, estimated SPLIT (`65.8`) barely moves with GT Geometry alone (`68.6`, +2.8) or GT Grounding alone (`66.3`, +0.5), but jumps **+16.1 to 81.9** with **both**.
   - Grounding alone solves *appearance order* (`76→96`) and *counting* (`56.1→69.6`); geometry alone solves *room size* (`40.0→80.6`) and *route planning* (`68.9→77.8`); *absolute distance* (`59.6→85.8`), *object size* (`67.2→87.6`), and *relative direction* (`86.7→96.7`) demand **joint** grounding + depth. Notably, GT masks lifted through *noisy* depth hurt size/distance because border-clipped masks pull background points.
   - **Single-property physical specificity:** Fixing one per-scene metric scale scalar boosts *only* metric tasks (+30.7 cam displacement, +13.7 room size, +13.6 obj size, +11.9 cam-obj dist, +9.1 abs dist) while scale-invariant tasks stay ~0.0. Removing gravity alignment breaks *only* `+Z` tasks (−58.9 rel dir, −41.2 route, −7.6 obj size), and simply telling the VLM axes are unaligned recovers 83% of that loss.
3. **Cross-view instance identity or world-frame poses are non-negotiable (`X6` PREREG_182).**
   - Frame-local GT boxes without poses (`Arm NL`, `83.6`) secretly leak cross-view identity because exact `(L, W, H)` uniquely fingerprints **98.8%** of repeated objects. Stripping box sizes to frame-local centers (`NL-Centers`) drops **−9.4 pp** overall (`83.25→73.83`) and **−14.6 pp** on multi-instance questions (`83.97→69.37`, indistinguishable from video `65.34`). Adding camera poses (`Arm P`, `92.2`) and world registration (`Arm W`, `95.8`) cleanly restores multi-instance reasoning.
4. **Paired thought shifts prove offloading perception eliminates metric guessing and speeds up inference.**
   - Across 150 paired questions, SPLIT collapses metric/prior guessing (**M**: Pro `14.4→2.1%`, Flash `13.5→2.6%`) and pixel search (**V**: Pro `38.7→14.0%`, Flash `39.2→28.7%`), replacing them with geometric calculation (**G**: `23.1→35.8%`), verification (**C**: `10.0→22.9%` Pro, `5.8→20.1%` Flash), and tool planning (**T**: `1.5→13.3%`).
   - Thinking tokens drop **2.6×** (Pro) and **7.1×** (Flash); on short video SPLIT is **1.4× faster** (`20.2s` vs `24.8s`), uses **3.8× fewer tokens**, and costs less while beating Base at every thinking budget.
5. **Test-time scaling (Maj@8) cannot fix systematic perceptual illusions (`PILOT_REVSI_MAJ8`).**
   - Eight High-thinking Gemini 3.1 Pro samples (`55k` gen tokens, `$1.21/q`) raise the 3-bench mean from `61.9→66.9`, still **−9.0 pp behind single-pass SPLIT (`75.9`)** (ReVSI: Maj@8 `73.4` vs SPLIT `77.7`).
   - **61.0%** of Maj@8 errors have ≥6/8 samples agreeing on the *exact same wrong answer/side*. Base VLMs suffer systematic perceptual priors: 100% of Pro's camera displacements cluster on `0.5 m` multiples (`ρ=0.43` vs SPLIT `0.99`), >2/3 of room triples produce geometrically *impossible* left/right sets (SPLIT cuts this >3× to 92% accuracy), mirrored videos fail to flip left/right (`3–5×` more contradictions than SPLIT), and unseen viewpoints past 90° drop Pro −22% (SpaceCast `43.6→76.1`, Flash `59.4→85.1`; ViewSpatial `45.9→67.8`).
6. **Closed-loop verification is a recovery mechanism for noisy perception (`PILOT_ONESHOT_PROGRAM`), while Python-only adds almost nothing (`PYTHON_CONTROL`).**
   - Mandatory Python + SPLIT coordinate conventions without 3-D tools gains only **+2.0 pp** mean across 4 benchmarks vs SPLIT's **+14.4 pp** (and Think3D loses to unscaled point clouds [39%] and renders-only final turns [31%] that force re-reading pixels).
   - Single-shot Python programs calling SPLIT's *exact* tools execute cleanly (`99.1%` exit 0) yet score **63.6 vs SPLIT's 75.4 (−11.8 pp)** because they cannot recover from empty detections (`15.3%` of queries: `52.6` vs `72.0`) or disambiguate multi-instance tracks (`80.8%` of non-counting queries: `64.9` vs `78.1`). Rerunning those *unchanged* scripts with GT tools jumps them to **84.4 / 87.7** (matching SPLIT+GT `85.1 / 90.4`).
7. **Dynamic 4-D, Multi-Cam Ego-Exo, Long Video, and Causal VQ3D breakthroughs.**
   - **Dynamic 4-D (`Point4D + MapAnything`):** Beats Base in **12/12** cells across Pro, Gemma, Qwen (DSI **+27.0** Pro / **+22.2** Qwen; ReSTI **+16.3–20.7**; OSI **+3.4–6.4**). On reversed playback, Base repeats forward-motion priors ~50% of the time (reversed VSTI camera motion: Base gets both directions right in `10%` of rooms vs SPLIT **98%**). Even atop frontier **Gemini 4 Argon**, SPLIT still gains **+2 to +10 pp**, with 162/165 wins resting on 4-D geometry.
   - **Long Video (HD-EPIC 4–55 min & HourVideo dev-20):** Loop-closed streaming reconstruction (`HiP3R + SALAD + UniK3D`, **6.12 GB RSS / 10.5 GiB VRAM**, 4.7 video-min/GPU-min) keeps HD-EPIC accuracy flat across duration (**87.0** Pro / **88.6** Flash vs Base **53.2 / 66.0**; frame-tools without 3-D stall at `56.6`). On HourVideo, blackout-aware yaw stitching lifts single-coordinate-frame coverage from **1/20 → 19/20** videos (largest lit share `0.531 → 0.972`).
   - **Cross-View Ego-Exo4D (`T2`, n=210):** Video-only SPLIT hits **83.9 (2A cross-view) / 52.0 (2B mobile)** vs Base **39.3 / 30.6** (**+33.5 overall**, +44.6 on 2A, `p=8e-12`; GoPro–GoPro rotation `1.65°`, Aria–GoPro `2.26°`, Sim(3) ATE `0.05 m`). v2 overlap-rotation + yaw-continuity stitching cuts climbing heading error `35.2°→6.1°` and all-job ATE `1.12→0.56 m`.
   - **Causal 3-D Localization (`T3` VQ3D, 0≤t≤t_q):** Causal SPLIT cuts pose-free proxy median L2 from **1.56 m → 0.43 m** (Succ@1m `30.1% → 73.7%`), landing within 2 s of GT last occurrence on 99/163 queries.
   - **Supervised specialist warning (`sec:supp-specialists`):** VLM-3R, Cambrian-S, and Cambrian-P drop **11–22 pp** moving from VSIBench to ReVSI (same scans, repaired answerable questions), exposing heavy reliance on count-1/2 frequency priors and dataset shortcuts.

## 3. Where SPLIT Still Breaks (Master Plan & DIAG1–5 Synthesis)

1. **Systematic Static Over-Scale (`1.11×–1.52×` across 5 independent cohorts) & Scale–Extent Cancellation (`S1–S2`):**
   - Unpaired marginal median depth ratios (`median(UniK3D)/median(G3T)`) + running `UniK3D` with `camera=None` (ignoring reconstruction intrinsics) + portrait aspect-ratio stretch inflate linear scale by `1.11×–1.52×` (squaring into `1.24×–1.7×` room-area error on ReVSI/VSIBench and −40 pp on OST-Bench ARKit).
   - Worse, extent tools currently *shrink* objects ~20%, masking the over-scale (median size ratio `0.92–0.96`). Fixing scale alone regresses object size; fixing extents alone overshoots. Scale + oriented trimmed multi-view extents must be co-calibrated.
2. **Grounding Recall, Tiny Objects, Specular/Truncated Surfaces (`C2, C3, C8`):**
   - `SAM3` misses small objects (15–17 px in Ego-Exo exo views; 0 hits on VQ3D query labels at thresh 0.3), specular mirrors, and ambiguous part-vs-whole labels. In counting, `52/337` losses have `sam3_sum < GT`; raw tracker count scores `45.1` vs `69.6` with GT grounding.
   - Noun backoff / synonym union risks wrong-instance collisions (`14/51` held-out Ego-Exo views).
3. **Cross-Submap Fragmentation & Gravity-Less Close-Ups (`A9, C10`):**
   - Low overlap inliers on planar/close-range views (climbing, dark frames, top-down VQ3D scooter benches) fracture trajectories into disconnected submaps (`40.3` cross-submap vs `68.1` within-submap on Ego-Exo 2B). Floor RANSAC fails on most ego clips (`4/198` Ego-Exo submaps; most HourVideo submaps fall back to noisy cam-up).
4. **Dynamic 4-D Ceilings (`C5–C7`, Argon Analysis):**
   - Outdoor 4-D `MapAnything` scale runs `1.36×` median (OSI trajectory length 60 over / 3 under).
   - Noisy 3-D track jitter inflates stationary target speed (Pro `−35.9`, Flash `−18.2` on stationary OSI targets). Object facing axis / turn sign is only `69%` accurate on true turns. Even with Argon, `78/115` losses come from wrong tool geometry/grounding and `150/212` both-wrong items need better 4-D metric geometry.
5. **Multi-Turn Latency, Context Bloat, & Harness Fragility (`LONGVIDEO_SPEED`, `A1–A14`):**
   - On long video, 4+ High-thinking turns generate `3,669` tokens (2.9× Base) and take `2.1×` longer (`45.3s` vs `21.4s`), with 47% of episodes wasting an empty `print("Done")` round and hand-computing trig in prose. Re-sending images across 16 rounds balloons VQ3D prompts to `241k` tokens (`$0.1–$4.9/q`).
   - Cold SAM3 full-video scans cost `3.5–23 s/min` (CPU-bound). Small contract gaps (`sam3_vid` missing `centroid_xyz`, truncated JSON helpers, unaligned frame indices, 0-tool pseudo-calls) silently cost 3–10 pp.

## 4. Top 10 Direct Inspirations for Our Fine-Tuned Spatial Student + VLA Backbone

1. **Train a Joint Native 3-D/4-D Geometry + Cross-View Instance Identity Front-End—Never Frame-Local Centers Alone.**
   - *Evidence:* `tab:perception-parts` (+16.1 only when *both* grounding and geometry improve) + `X6` (frame-local centers without cross-view identity collapse −14.6 pp on multi-instance tasks).
   - *Design:* Attach metric Z-up pointmap + camera pose (`SE(3)` gravity-aligned) + persistent 3-D instance-ID/track heads directly to the student's visual encoder (distilling `G3T/Point4D + UniK3D(known-K) + SAM3`), so every visual token carries metric `(X,Y,Z)`, gravity alignment, and cross-frame entity correspondence natively inside forward pass.
2. **Co-Calibrate Metric Scale (Paired-Pixel + Known Intrinsics) Jointly with Oriented 3-D Extents.**
   - *Evidence:* `S1–S2` (unpaired median scale + `camera=None` causes 1.11×–1.52× bias across 5 cohorts, cancelling −20% extent shrinkage) + `tab:supp-swaps` (true scale lifts metric tasks +9 to +31 pp).
   - *Design:* Supervise the student's depth/scale head with paired common-valid-pixel metric targets conditioned on exact camera intrinsics (aspect-preserving, pad-masked), and jointly supervise oriented 3-D bounding boxes / closest-surface distances so scale and extent errors never cancel out in training.
3. **Bake Explicit Gravity Alignment (`+Z` Up) & Egocentric Polar Readouts (`bearing_deg`, `range_m`, `Δpose`) into the Student's Latent/Tool Interface.**
   - *Evidence:* Removing gravity drops direction −58.9 pp (`tab:supp-swaps`); precomputing egocentric `bearing_deg` + `horizontal_distance_m` (`L12`) eliminates 2,100 tokens of hand trig and cuts latency in half; camera-frame `(forward, right, up)` localization is the exact primitive VLAs need.
   - *Design:* For both spatial QA and VLA action heads, expose a canonical gravity-leveled egocentric polar representation `(r, bearing, Δz, relative_yaw)` from the student's own pose/depth heads—eliminating multi-step coordinate arithmetic from the LM weights and giving VLA policies direct metric action targets.
4. **Distill SPLIT's Paired Thought Shift into Compact, Grounded CoT (Zero Metric Guessing, High Verification).**
   - *Evidence:* `GEMINI_PAIRED_THOUGHTS` (M guessing `14.4%→2.1%`, thinking tokens `2.6×–7.1×` lower) + `X1` (8B needs thinking: `+26.1 pp` over non-thinking!).
   - *Design:* Collect API-teacher traces (Gemini Pro/Flash + SPLIT / SPLIT+GT) filtered to strictly eliminate `M` (metric guessing) and `V` (blind pixel-search loops) while preserving `G` (geometric composition) and `C` (consistency verification). For an 8B–9B student, teach short, deterministic geometric CoT over its own heads' readouts (think only when composing or verifying, mimicking `L9` Low-thinking later turns + `L11` answer-when-ready).
5. **Build a Streaming Loop-Closed 3-D Spatial Memory Bank for Long-Horizon Videos & Embodied Navigation.**
   - *Evidence:* `HD-EPIC` (`87.0` flat at 55 min vs Base `53.2` decay), `HourVideo` (`6 GB` streaming `HiP3R+SALAD`, yaw-continuity stitching `19/20` single-frame), `VQ3D` causal memory (`0.43 m` vs `1.56 m`).
   - *Design:* Give the student/VLA a causal, bounded-RAM streaming 3-D spatial memory (keyframe world poses + metric feature-pointmaps + loop-closure / yaw-continuity bridging across turns, hard-cut on true blackouts) instead of naive uniform frame subsampling. Query it via two-stage retrieval: cheap dense semantic index (SigLIP-style) over all past timestamps → high-res re-inspection of only the top-K relevant moments.
6. **Decouple Low-Cost "Probe" Visual Attention from High-Res "Verify" Attention (Two-Budget Architecture).**
   - *Evidence:* `HourVideo` (`1,024` probe / `256` planner budget), `VQ3D` (`192` Flash-LOW crop-probe / `96` planner budget), and `PILOT_ONESHOT_PROGRAM` (+11.8 pp from closed-loop verification of ambiguous/empty tracks).
   - *Design:* Give the student an internal two-resolution / two-step visual mechanism: fast coarse scan across 64–1,000+ frames using compressed visual tokens + 3-D heads, followed by an active high-res crop/re-focus pass *only* when head confidence is low, zero instances are found, or ≥2 same-category instances compete.
7. **Train Closed-Loop Self-Verification via RL on Head-Backed Rollouts (Recovering from Imperfect Own Heads).**
   - *Evidence:* `PILOT_ONESHOT_PROGRAM` (one-shot scripts match SPLIT+GT when tools are perfect, but lose −11.8 pp when perception is noisy) + `MMSI` text-only vs closed-loop (`57.2→67.8`) + `S3` (advisory flags fail, direct verification against pixels works).
   - *Design:* After SFT on clean head-using CoT, run RL (GRPO/verifiable spatial rewards) with the student querying its *actual* (noisy) internal heads + RGB frames so it learns *when* a predicted mask/depth is truncated, specular, or wrong-instance—and actively re-queries another frame or viewpoint rather than blindly trusting a single noisy readout.
8. **Enforce Physical & Geometric Invariance Augmentation to Avoid Specialist Overfitting (Cambrian-P / VLM-3R Trap).**
   - *Evidence:* Supervised specialists drop **11–22 pp** on ReVSI (answerable questions on the *same* scans), overfit count-1/2 priors, fail left-right mirrored videos, and fail reversed playback (`10%` vs `98%` bidirectional VSTI).
   - *Design:* Make **exact geometric consistency** a first-class training objective: train on paired left-right mirrored videos (swapping left/right labels), temporally reversed clips (inverting camera/object motion), all-6-permutation room triples (enforcing cycle-consistent 3-D layouts), large unseen viewpoint rotations (`>90°`) + off-path translation flips (`T1b`), and high-count / ReVSI-clean answerable distributions.
9. **Unify Static 3-D, Dynamic 4-D, and Multi-Cam Cross-View Registration in One Geometry Backbone.**
   - *Evidence:* `Point4D` unlocks +16–27 pp on dynamic videos; Ego-Exo4D cross-view registration (`1.65°–2.26°` rotation, `0.05 m` ATE) unlocks **+44.6 pp** on ego↔exo reasoning—directly transferable to multi-camera robots (wrist + overhead + third-person cameras) and moving humans/objects.
   - *Design:* Train the geometry encoder jointly on static scans, dynamic 4-D scenes (separating camera ego-motion from moving object tracks with jitter-robust kinematics), and uncalibrated multi-camera ego-exo views so one VLA backbone natively maps wrist, head, and third-person views into a single metric world frame.
10. **Turn SPLIT's Perception-Gap Diagnostic into an Automated Data-Flywheel & Curriculum Engine.**
    - *Evidence:* SPLIT+GT reaches `90–99%` on sampled frames; SPLIT beats Base by `+22–45 pp` specifically on never-co-visible, unseen-viewpoint (`>90°`), long-video, and cross-view questions—and generates exact, verifiable 3-D intermediate supervision (`P_i`, `T_i`, tracks, bearings, clearances, trajectories) from unlabeled RGB video.
    - *Design:* Use SPLIT (and SPLIT+GT where 3-D scans exist) as our **auto-labeling teacher** to mine hard, high-signal training questions (never-co-visible, >90° viewpoint shift, multi-instance disambiguation, dynamic motion, long-horizon causal localization) and distill *both* dense 3-D head targets and verified head-backed CoT traces at scale—targeting the exact perception bottlenecks where end-to-end VLMs fail.
