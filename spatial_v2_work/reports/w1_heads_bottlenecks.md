### True Physical/Architectural Ceilings in Pose, Depth & Towers
1. **Frozen SigLIP-lineage Qwen ViT erases multi-view correspondence (`POSE_PLATEAU_DIAGNOSIS`):** Per-frame Qwen ViT merger tokens yield chance pose (`58°–77°` dir, `71°–89°` rot), **remain at chance (`63.8°–72.2°` dir, `72°–92°` rot) under an 8-layer cross-frame token transformer (`G=8`)**, and **remain at chance (`64.6°–70.5°`) at 2x pixel resolution (`R2` probe; trained `RES2` `0.93x` loss)**. Contrastive semantic pretraining discards sub-patch projective ray phase; `G3T`/`DA3` preserve epipolar correspondence via alternating frame/global dense attention trained end-to-end.
2. **Causal LM synthesis is the bottleneck — NOT `1`-token `CAM` compression, head size, or overfitting:**
   * At LM layer 16, probing all `4,160` visual tokens (`28.8°–30.1°` dir) ties probing 1 `CAM` token/frame (`29.6°–32.7°`), reaching `15.3°–20.4°` at final `CAM` vs `7.5°–10.9°` on `G3T` camera tokens. Because causal attention blocks frame $t$ from attending to $t'>t$ until trailing carriers, the hybrid GDN/attention LM must synthesize correspondence from semantic tokens across 32 layers.
   * Adding trainable vision branches in LM layers 1–8 (`IF-P0`) nudged probes only `18.11° -> 15.50°` (bar `<=5°`).
   * Not generalization or capacity: `T0` missed its *own* training clips by `11.5°–14.0°` dir (`28%–32%` centre), whereas 50-scene overfit (`pdO`, 12 epochs) reached `5.7°` dir / `6.3°` rot / `12%` centre (labels exact within `0.06°`).
3. **Loss-only teacher alignment (`C1-r`) buys speed, zero ceiling:** Aligning final `CAM` states to `G3T` layer-23 tokens (`Pearson + CCC`) cut rotation error `-19.6°` at m100 and `-8.1°` at m200, but hit the exact `C1-a` centre-error plateau by m400 (`58%` vs `61%`; `49%` at m800). Conversely, **injecting `G3T` features at the `CAM` input (`GXC`)** halved direction error (`20.1° -> 9.8°` at m200).
4. **Own trainable ViT (`V-C`) solves metric scale, not multi-view depth shape (`SHARED_PERCEPTION`, `K1-X`):** Detaching a trainable `0.45B` ViT copy (`V-C`, taps 9/17/25, FiLM FoV) cut `AbsRel` from `0.29` to `0.10–0.16` in 400 steps (vs 2,800 steps for shared-LM carriers) and **solved metric scale**: `s = median(own_depth / G3T_depth)` hits `served/GT = 1.011` on K1 (`83.8` disp MRA vs `46.1` constant-scale `G3T` and `87.8` per-capture GT ceiling; GT scale scores `-1.4 [-6.0, +2.3]` vs deployed on K1 held-out). Own depth shape (`0.13` vs `0.08` `G3T` obj-pixel `AbsRel`) cost `-6.5 [-9.7, -3.4]` pts on VSI, while under `lift_bestspread` (`51x51` grid, 1 frame) `K1-X` found `79.1` vs `E3`'s dense multi-view `92.0` (`-12.9` pts lost inside the single-frame `51x51` lift rule itself). Own gravity errs `1.32°` on ScanNet/++ and `5.46°` on ARKit.
5. **Why `G3T` STILL failed on `32/38` `dev-S1` GT captures (`TOWER_CAMERA_FAILURES`, `K5-S`):**
   * Across the `38` GT captures of `dev-S1` routed distance (`23` ARKit, `11` ScanNet++, `4` ScanNet), **only `6/38` pass** rigid alignment (`RMS <= 0.10 m`, all anchor rotations `<= 5°`); **`32/38` fail** and **`10/38` are gross (`max rot > 60°`: `8` ARKit, `2` ScanNet++)**. **ARKit fails on `21/23` captures** (`2` pass, `13` moderate, `8` gross; ScanNet/++ `4/9/2`).
   * GT frame binding (same-frame mask IoU `0.618` vs `0.083` shifted), portrait orientation (`AUC 0.517`), and axis conventions checked clean. **Doubling `G3T` from 32 to 63 frames (`Orchard 173701`) repaired `0/5` gross ARKit captures** (`34.4° -> 36.2°` rel-rot RMS).
   * **Physical/numerical root cause:** `G3T` is **ill-conditioned** on fast-turn, low-overlap handheld ARKit clips (`192x256` `lowres_wide` bicubic-upscaled `2.5x`; `>1 m` translation gaps separate gross vs pass at `AUC 0.858`). A `~1/255` decode delta or cuBLAS default-vs-deterministic path (`K5-S`) swings gross cameras by **`1.1°–17.9°`** (`<1°` on passing; disagreement separates gross vs pass at **`AUROC = 1.000`**; sharing the CUDA process with the LM shifted ARKit cameras up to `0.141 m` in `Rank 1`). Pairwise distances hid global rigid drift on nearby anchors, but on ARKit cross-anchor pairs (`DEV-MASK` `g - e`), GT relative pose adds **`+15.1` pts** (`g - a = +22.1 [+3.2, +43.8]` vs `e - a = +7.0`). On well-conditioned captures, remaining camera errors sit at `<5°` turns (`12.5/16.8` rot loss pts from whole-degree rounding vs MRA) and `<=0.5 m` moves (`11.5/18.1` disp loss pts).

1. **Selector `null` veto + zero-failure pool -> `136–198`-call loops (`H1-0`, step 544):** `0/73,088` pool rows showed `"not seen"`. Dropping `null` on nonempty pools cut `"not seen"` `74 -> 7/294` and caps to `0`.
2. **Metre-only & narrow-wording brittleness -> `62/150` skipped calls (`H1-0`, `R1` m200):** Metre-only routed rows caused `H1-0`/step-544 to copy raw metres onto `101/146` non-metre `dev-S1` items (`cm` `-43.9` pts; `5.0 -> 73.2` after unit rows, though official benchmarks ask metres).
