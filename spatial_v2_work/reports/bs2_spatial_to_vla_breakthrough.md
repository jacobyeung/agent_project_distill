# Blue-Sky Round 2B: Making a Geometric Spatial Model the Ultimate VLA Pretraining Backbone

**Target:** Transform our `DA3+EpiAdapter` / `Point4D` / `ScaleAnchor` / `Qwen3.5-9B` spatial model from a passive 3D QA engine into the strongest manipulation (`LIBERO-Spatial` 5-demo, `LIBERO-Plus +Cam`) and navigation (`R2R-CE`) foundation backbone ever built.
**Core Insight from 2026-11-28 Data:** Standard VLM-to-VLA transfer fails because 2D visual tokens entangle camera intrinsics/extrinsics (`1.94×` Habitat FoV drift; `+Cam` collapse), lack contact-induced 3D dynamics, ignore online geometric failure (`reproj_p90` detects it in `2.98 ms` at `0.898 AUROC`), and corrupt hybrid `GatedDeltaNet` recurrent states (`48` `bf16` `A_log`/`norm.weight` tensors blew up at step 3). Six radical architectural leaps invert every one of those failure modes into a structural VLA advantage.

---

## Idea 1: `ScaleAnchor`-Equivariant SE(3) Canonical Token Field (Zero-Shot `+Cam` & FoV Immunity)

1. **Radical Thesis:** Stop feeding camera-frame 2D patch tokens into action heads and hoping camera augmentation teaches viewpoint invariance. Instead, lift every observation through predicted depth, camera pose, and an explicit metric `ScaleAnchor`, then **re-project visual features onto a fixed robot-base 3D canonical token grid** before the policy head ever sees them—making arbitrary camera extrinsics (`+Cam`) and FoV shifts (`60° ↔ 90°`) mathematically invisible.
2. **Exact Mechanism & Interface (`vla_libero` & `navrun`):**
   - **Focal-Equivariant Depth + Differentiable `ScaleAnchor` Slot:** Predict ray-normalized depth $\tilde{D}_t(u,v) = D_t(u,v) \cdot (f_{\text{ref}}/f_t)$ from `DA3` DINOv2-L + `EpiAdapter` (`AbsRel 0.084`, `d1 94.1%`, `grav 4.78°`), then multiply by an online anchor scalar $s^*$ solved in closed form from known robot/agent physics: wrist FK baseline $\Delta\mathbf{t}^{\text{FK}}$ or gripper jaw width in manipulation, and gravity-leveled camera height $h_{\text{cam}} = 1.25\text{ m}$ in Habitat (`0.42%` residual error, eliminating the `tan 45°/tan 29° = 1.94×` FoV bias).
   - **Canonical Base-Frame 3D Cross-Attention Resampler (`CanonField3D`):** Define a fixed set of $M = 128$ learnable 3D query anchors $\mathbf{Q}^{\mathcal{B}} \in \mathbb{R}^{M \times 3}$ spanning the robot workspace ($\mathcal{B}$: `[-0.5, 0.8] × [-0.6, 0.6] × [0.0, 0.7] m`) or egocentric gravity-leveled nav fan (`r ∈ [0.4, 5.0] m`). Unproject every patch from `agentview` and `wrist` into $\mathcal{B}$ using $\hat{\mathbf{T}}_{\mathcal{B}\leftarrow\text{cam}} = \mathbf{T}_{\mathcal{B}\leftarrow\text{wrist}}(\mathbf{q}_t)\hat{\mathbf{T}}_{\text{wrist}\leftarrow\text{agent}}(s^*)$ (respecting MuJoCo's `fx < 0` mirror). Cross-attend $\mathbf{Q}^{\mathcal{B}}$ to unprojected patch features with Metric 3D-RoPE keys $[\mathbf{P}^{\mathcal{B}}, \mathbf{r}^{\mathcal{B}}, \log\hat{D}, p^{\text{stat}}]$ and `25%` random view-drop during pretraining.
   - **`vla_libero` (`QwenOFT`/`FlowExpert`):** Feed the canonical `geo_tokens` `[B, 128, d]` directly into `FlowExpert.precompute_memory(..., geo_tokens=canon_tokens)` (and replace raw `agentview` patch embeddings in `QwenOFT` with `canon_tokens + wrist_tokens`), eliminating the brittle external `ViewSetG3T` bottleneck.
   - **`navrun` (`R2R-CE`):** Sample the canonical gravity-leveled polar floor grid `[B, 128, d]` anchored by `h_cam`; `ClickTranslator` decodes waypoints directly in metric polar `(range_m, bearing_deg)` instead of FoV-sensitive pixel depth.
3. **Why It Beats Standard VLM-to-VLA Transfer (5-Demo & `+Cam`):** Under any camera rotation/translation $\mathbf{T}_{\text{shift}}$ or FoV change $K'$, the unprojected 3D pointset $\mathbf{P}^{\mathcal{B}}$ is identically invariant ($\hat{\mathbf{T}}_{\mathcal{B}\leftarrow\text{cam}}\mathbf{T}_{\text{shift}} \cdot \mathbf{T}_{\text{shift}}^{-1}\mathbf{P}^{\text{cam}} = \mathbf{P}^{\mathcal{B}}$). A `5-demo` policy trained from *one* viewpoint generalizes **zero-shot** to extreme `LIBERO-Plus +Cam` perturbations without ever seeing `+Cam` demos, and transfers `60°` ScanNet pretraining straight to `90°` Habitat `R2R-CE`.
4. **Decisive Fast Proof Test & Kill Criterion:** Freeze `DA3+EpiAdapter` + `CanonField3D`, train `5-demo` `LIBERO-Spatial` (`d5_s0`, 50 demos, `10k` steps, `4× H100`, `4.5h`) with zero `+Cam` augmentation, evaluate on `376` `LIBERO-Plus +Cam` episodes; plus `100` `R2R-CE` frames with `90°`/`110°` FoV sweep. **Kill if** `+Cam` SR drops `> 6 pp` relative to in-distribution `Spatial` SR or beats `B1` by `< +20 pp` on `+Cam`, or if `110°` FoV `ClickTranslator` leg error exceeds `0.15 m`.

## Idea 2: "Action-is-Object-Flow" — Cross-View Human-to-Robot 4D Contact-Flow Pretraining

1. **Radical Thesis:** Five robot demos are too few to learn 3D object physics and manipulation geometry from scratch, yet internet-scale human video (`Ego-Exo4D`, `HD-EPIC`) contains millions of metric 3D object interactions. Pretrain our spatial backbone as an **embodiment-agnostic 4D Contact-Flow World Model** that predicts future ego-decoupled 3D object displacement $\Delta\mathbf{P}^{\mathcal{W}}_{t\to t+H}$ identically from ego or exo cameras—turning downstream VLA fine-tuning from "learn manipulation" into thin "track the predicted 3D contact trajectory."
2. **Exact Mechanism & Interface (`vla_libero` & `navrun`):**
   - **Ego-Decoupled 4D Contact-Flow Teacher (`Point4D` + `SPLIT` Cross-View):** Run `Point4D` (`0.05–0.58 m/s` moving objects cleanly isolated against `0.02 m` static background under `15–53 m` camera motion) on synchronized `Ego-Exo4D` (`aria` + 4 exo cams) and `HD-EPIC` streaming clips. Extract for every task-relevant moving region ($p^{\text{dyn}} > 0.5$): (a) **3D Contact Point Trajectory** $\mathbf{c}^{\mathcal{W}}_{t:t+H}$ (centroid of hand-object interface, scale-locked via SLAM + `8.4 cm` hand breadth), (b) **Rigid/Articulated Object Screw Flow** $(\mathbf{v}^{\mathcal{W}}, \boldsymbol{\omega}^{\mathcal{W}})$ over horizons $\delta \in \{1, 4, 16, 32\}$, and (c) **Cross-View Consistency Loss** forcing `EpiAdapter` + LLM taps `{16, 24}` fed *only* an exo cam to predict the exact same world-frame 3D contact flow as when fed the ego `aria` cam (extending `SPLIT`'s `+33.5 pp` cross-view win to 4D dynamics).
   - **`vla_libero` (`FlowExpert`/`QwenOFT`):** Add a pre-trained `ContactFlowHead` on taps `{16, 24}` emitting $H=16$ future 3D contact-point waypoints + object-motion tokens `[B, 16, 12]` (3D position, velocity, principal approach normal, rigidity). Condition `FlowExpert` (`t_goal` / `geo_tokens` input) on this predicted 3D contact-flow tube; during 5-demo LIBERO FT, `FlowExpert` predicts the robot EEF trajectory that matches the pre-trained object contact flow plus gripper open/close.
   - **`navrun` (`R2R-CE`):** Predict future 3D ego-camera trajectory flow $\Delta\mathbf{T}^{\mathcal{W}}_{t\to t+H}$ (`0.5–3.0 m` lookahead) from `HD-EPIC` / `ScanNet++` / `MP3D-train` walkthroughs directly in gravity-leveled space, feeding `navrun`'s `controller_v2` continuous `(dx, dy, dyaw)` subgoals before seeing a single R2R instruction.
3. **Why It Beats Standard VLM-to-VLA Transfer (5-Demo & `+Cam`):** Standard VLMs know *what* a mug or drawer is, not *how its 3D pointcloud moves when picked or pulled*. Pretraining cross-view 4D contact flow endows taps `{16, 24}` with physically grounded 3D affordance trajectories independent of robot morphology or camera angle; 5 demos only calibrate the thin `hand-contact → Franka-EEF` offset.
4. **Decisive Fast Proof Test & Kill Criterion:** Pretrain `ContactFlowHead` + LoRA on `2,000` `Ego-Exo4D` + `HD-EPIC` clips (`Point4D` labels, `~12` H100-h), then run `1-demo` and `5-demo` `LIBERO-Spatial` (`d1_s0`, `d5_s0`) comparing `FlowExpert` with vs without `ContactFlowHead` conditioning. **Kill if** `ContactFlowHead` adds `< +8 pp` SR on `1-demo` LIBERO-Spatial or `< +5 pp` on `5-demo`, or if held-out `Ego-Exo4D` exo-to-ego 3D contact-flow endpoint error exceeds `12 cm`.

## Idea 3: Metric 3D Analytic-Plus-Residual Action Heads (`SE(3)` Geodesic Prior $\oplus$ Neural Residual)

1. **Radical Thesis:** Asking a neural network to regress raw 7-DoF delta poses from pixels wastes 90% of scarce 5-demo capacity re-learning Euclidean subtraction (`target - eef`) and pinhole unprojection. Decompose every robot action into an **analytic metric 3D geometric transport primitive** computed directly by our spatial heads plus a **bounded `se(3)` neural residual** from `FlowExpert`/`QwenOFT`.
2. **Exact Mechanism & Interface (`vla_libero` & `navrun`):**
   - **Manipulation (`vla_libero` `FlowExpert` / `QwenOFT`):** From the spatial model's predicted target 3D contact pose $\hat{\mathbf{T}}^{\mathcal{B}}_{\text{contact}} \in SE(3)$ (lifted via `DA3+EpiAdapter` + `ScaleAnchor` + Idea 2's contact-flow head) and exact proprioception $\mathbf{T}^{\mathcal{B}}_{\text{ee}}(\mathbf{q}_t)$, compute an analytic geodesic `SE(3)` transport chunk of length $H=8$ using `spatial_v2/geo_heads.py:se3_log` / `se3_exp`:
     $$\boldsymbol{\xi}^{\text{geo}}_k = \text{clip}\!\left(\gamma_k \cdot \text{se3\_log}\big(\hat{\mathbf{T}}^{\mathcal{B}}_{\text{contact}} (\mathbf{T}^{\mathcal{B}}_{\text{ee}})^{-1}\big),\, \mathbf{v}_{\max}\right), \qquad \mathbf{a}_k = \boldsymbol{\xi}^{\text{geo}}_k \oplus \Delta\mathbf{a}^{\text{res}}_k(\text{FlowExpert}),$$
     where `FlowExpert` predicts only the bounded contact/avoidance residual $\Delta\mathbf{a}^{\text{res}}_k \in [-5\text{ cm}, +5\text{ cm}] \times [-15^\circ, +15^\circ]$ plus gripper logit, initialized near zero (`action_out` zero-init) with a learned phase gate $\alpha_t \in [0,1]$ that hands authority from analytic transit (`\alpha ≈ 1`) to learned fine contact (`\alpha ≈ 0`) when $\|\mathbf{x}_{\text{target}} - \mathbf{x}_{\text{ee}}\| < 6\text{ cm}$.
   - **Navigation (`navrun` `R2R-CE`):** Compute the analytic collision-free geodesic step `(r_geo, θ_geo)` directly from the `ScaleAnchor`-calibrated `DA3` depth obstacle map + gravity `floor_plane` lift to the VLM's predicted 3D subgoal, and let a lightweight `nav_residual` head predict only `(Δr, Δθ, stop_logit)` to handle tight doorways, stairs, and semantic stopping—bypassing quantized `15°/0.25m` dead-ends.
3. **Why It Beats Standard VLM-to-VLA Transfer (5-Demo & `+Cam`):** At **step 0 of VLA fine-tuning**, the policy already servoes cleanly toward any named 3D object or room waypoint from arbitrary viewpoints without memorizing workspace coordinates! With `5 demos`, the neural head spends 100% of its capacity on contact mechanics (grasp alignment, placement compliance, obstacle clearance) instead of spatial coordinate fitting.
4. **Decisive Fast Proof Test & Kill Criterion:** Compare `Residual-FlowExpert` vs standard `FlowExpert` (`vla_libero` `d1_s0` & `d5_s0`, `500` Spatial + `376` `+Cam` eps) and `Residual-Nav` vs `ClickTranslator` on `rand100` `R2R-CE`. **Kill if** `Residual-FlowExpert` fails to reach `>= 30%` grasp-approach reach rate at step `500` (5× faster convergence than standard `FlowExpert`) or fails to beat standard `FlowExpert` by `>= +7 pp` on `1-demo` LIBERO-Spatial or `>= +5 pp` SR on `rand100`.

## Idea 4: Closed-Loop `reproj_p90` + FK `ScaleAnchor` Active Self-Calibration During Rollouts

1. **Radical Thesis:** Today's `KT-1b` proved blind self-depth `TT-BA` hurts (`+0.48°` worse than `DA3-only`), while zero-GT `reproj_p90` detects geometric failure in **`2.98 ms` at `0.898 AUROC`**. Never run blind open-loop refinement—use `reproj_p90` as a **30 Hz closed-loop epistemic monitor** inside the rollout loop that dynamically triggers `ScaleAnchor` re-locking, sensor re-weighting, or a 1-step active parallax micro-probe whenever vision-geometry consistency breaks.
2. **Exact Mechanism & Interface (`vla_libero` & `navrun`):**
   - **2.98 ms Zero-GT Geometry Watchdog (`GeoWatchdog`):** At every chunk boundary, evaluate static-masked ($p^{\text{stat}} > 0.5$) multi-view reprojection `reproj_p90` between `(agentview_t, wrist_t, wrist_{t-8})` (manipulation) or `(frame_t, frame_{t-4})` (navigation), plus `scale_drift = |s_FK - s_pred| / s_FK`.
   - **Three-Tier Closed-Loop Recovery Policy (zero GT required):**
     1. *Nominal (`reproj_p90 <= τ_lo`):* Execute `DA3+EpiAdapter` fused canonical field at full 30 Hz (no BA overhead).
     2. *Camera Bump / Scale Drift (`τ_lo < reproj_p90 <= τ_hi`, e.g. mid-episode `+Cam` perturbation or Habitat floor slope):* Immediately flush cached `T_{B←agent}`, solve fresh `ScaleAnchor` $s^*$ from live FK wrist buffer (`Δt^FK >= 1.5 cm`) or gravity floor-plane (`h_cam`), and smoothly anneal `agentview` attention weight `w_exo = exp(-β · max(0, reproj_p90 - τ_lo))` in `FlowExpert`'s `memory_mask` so control degrades gracefully to FK-exact wrist vision (or floor-plane geometry in `R2R-CE`).
     3. *Severe Occlusion / Degeneracy (`reproj_p90 > τ_hi` or `p_vis < 0.2`):* Emit a single deterministic **Active Parallax Micro-Probe** — a `±3 cm` wrist lateral jiggle (creating fresh FK baseline for exact `ScaleAnchor` + triangulation) or a `±15°` Habitat yaw glance — then re-run `GeoHeads` before committing the action chunk.
   - **Interface:** Wrap `FlowExpert.sample_actions()` and `navrun`'s policy step with a stateless `GeoWatchdog` hook passing `memory_mask` soft confidence weights and an optional `probe_override` action primitive (`<4 ms` total latency).
3. **Why It Beats Standard VLM-to-VLA Transfer (5-Demo & `+Cam`):** Standard VLAs fail silently and irreversibly on mid-episode camera bumps, shiny/occluded tabletops, or Habitat doorway depth glitches because they have no scalar self-verification signal. `GeoWatchdog` converts a verified `0.898 AUROC` spatial-head diagnostic into **self-healing closed-loop embodiment**.
4. **Decisive Fast Proof Test & Kill Criterion:** Inject mid-episode random camera jolts (`±15°` yaw / `±10 cm` shift at step `t = 40` on `100` `LIBERO-Plus` eps) and `20%` random depth-scale corruption on `rand100` `R2R-CE`. **Kill if** `GeoWatchdog` detects `< 85%` of injected jolts within `2` chunks, adds `> 5 ms` median latency, or recovers `< +15 pp` SR on mid-episode jolts vs open-loop `U1`/`FlowExpert`.

## Idea 5: SE(3)-Transported `fp32` `GatedDeltaNet` — Differentiable 3D SLAM-Control Memory

1. **Radical Thesis:** Today's `m544` failure (`48` `linear_attn.{A_log, norm.weight}` tensors exported in `bf16` exploding at step 3) exposed that `Qwen3.5-9B`'s **24 GatedDeltaNet layers** are treated as fragile 1D text RNNs rather than geometric memory. Fix the `fp32` recurrent invariant AND upgrade DeltaNet's state update with **SE(3) relative pose transport** so the backbone's fixed-size recurrent matrix $\mathbf{S}_t \in \mathbb{R}^{d_k \times d_v}$ physically functions as an **O(1)-memory differentiable 3D SLAM & affordance map** across 500-frame rollouts.
2. **Exact Mechanism & Interface (`vla_libero` & `navrun`):**
   - **Strict `fp32` Recurrent Kernel Hygiene (Zero-Explosion Contract):** Enforce permanent `fp32` master & export storage for all `48` `linear_attn.{A_log, dt_bias, norm.weight}` tensors, plus clamp decay $\log\alpha_t = -\text{softplus}(\text{A\_log})\cdot \Delta_t \in [-8, -10^{-4}]$ so VLM train-mode VLA fine-tuning with gradient checkpointing has `0` non-finite steps across any horizon.
   - **SE(3)-Transported DeltaNet State Update (`GeoDeltaNet`):** Standard DeltaNet updates $\mathbf{S}_t = \alpha_t \mathbf{S}_{t-1} + \beta_t \mathbf{k}_t \mathbf{v}_t^\top$, which blurs 3D spatial associations whenever the camera/robot moves because $\mathbf{k}_t$ lives in a shifting frame. Using the predicted relative camera pose $\Delta\mathbf{T}_{t\leftarrow t-1} = (\Delta R_t, \Delta\mathbf{t}_t)$ from `GeoPoseHead`, apply a lightweight block-diagonal **orthogonal SE(3) key-transport operator** $\mathbf{R}_{\text{geo}}(\Delta\mathbf{T}_{t\leftarrow t-1})$ to the recurrent state at layers `13..31`:
     $$\mathbf{S}_t = \alpha_t \big(\mathbf{R}_{\text{geo}}(\Delta\mathbf{T}_{t\leftarrow t-1})\,\mathbf{S}_{t-1}\big) + \beta_t \mathbf{k}_t(\mathbf{P}^{\mathcal{B}}_t)\,\mathbf{v}_t^\top,$$
     where $\mathbf{R}_{\text{geo}}$ is an exact block-2D Givens/RoPE rotation over `(Δx, Δy, Δz, Δyaw)` (`O(d)` ops, norm-preserving = strictly stable eigenvalues `|λ| = 1`, zero extra VRAM!).
   - **`navrun` (`R2R-CE`) & `vla_libero`:** In `R2R-CE`, a 200-step navigation episode runs at constant `O(1)` KV memory while preserving exact metric 3D room memory across `180°` turns and multi-room backtracking (matching `HD-EPIC` `87.0` streaming without re-encoding 256 frames!). In `vla_libero`, multi-stage tasks (*"put mug in drawer, close drawer, stack bowl"*) retain occluded object poses in $\mathbf{S}_t$ even when the wrist camera looks away.
3. **Why It Beats Standard VLM-to-VLA Transfer (5-Demo & `+Cam`):** Standard attention VLAs either truncate history to 1–2 frames (blind to occluded objects and navigation backtracking) or explode compute quadratically on long streams. `GeoDeltaNet` turns `Qwen3.5-9B`'s hybrid architecture from a numerical liability into an **unmatched streaming 3D world-state advantage** that full-attention VLMs cannot match at 30 Hz.
4. **Decisive Fast Proof Test & Kill Criterion:** First verify `0` NaNs over `1,000` steps of `train_b1_544_gc.sh` with the `fp32` `A_log`/`norm.weight` fix. Then attach zero-init `R_geo` transport on `8` upper DeltaNet layers (`L17..24`), fine-tune on `HD-EPIC` (`152` Qs) + `10%` `R2R-CE` (`rand100`, 100+ step rollouts) + occlusion-heavy `LIBERO-Long` (`5-demo`). **Kill if** `GeoDeltaNet` adds `> 3%` step latency, causes any grad-norm spike `> 2×` baseline, or gains `< +4 pp` SR on `R2R-CE` backtracking episodes / `< +5 pp` on `LIBERO-Long` vs standard `fp32` DeltaNet.

## Idea 6: Unified Spatial-QA & Embodied-Control Latent Tokens ("Geo-Control Slots" $\mathcal{Z}^{\text{geo}}$)

1. **Radical Thesis:** Today, Spatial QA (`VSI`/`ReVSI`/`Ego-Exo4D`) and VLA control (`QwenOFT`/`FlowExpert`) fight over LLM weights—action FT wipes out grounding in `20k` steps while prose CoT fails to help `B1` on LIBERO. Force **both** Spatial QA reasoning and motor control to read and write through **one shared, compact bottleneck of $K = 40$ metric 3D latent tokens $\mathcal{Z}^{\text{geo}}$**—converting Spatial QA and VLA fine-tuning from zero-sum interference into **positive bidirectional transfer**.
2. **Exact Mechanism & Interface (`vla_libero` & `navrun`):**
   - **Fixed-Schema 3D Latent Interface (`GeoControlSlots`, `K = 40` tokens at `L16` & `L24`):**
     - `1` **Ego-Scale Slot** (`T_{B←cam}`, `log_s*`, `grav`, `reproj_p90`),
     - `31` **Entity/Waypoint 3D Slots** (each storing `[μ_B (3), obb_extent (3), yaw (2), presence_logit, semantic_emb]`, supervised by `Own-Box-3D` / `SAM 3` masklets on ScanNet/ARKit/DROID),
     - `8` **4D Contact-Flow / Subgoal Slots** (future 3D motion vectors from Idea 2 + active System-2 subgoal `T_goal^B`).
   - **Shared Consumer Contract:**
     - *Spatial QA Path:* VSI/ReVSI/Ego-Exo4D/HD-EPIC heads & short CoT programs attend *exclusively* to $\mathcal{Z}^{\text{geo}}$ (plus text) to answer distance, size, camera-motion, and cross-view correspondence questions.
     - *`vla_libero` (`FlowExpert` / `QwenOFT`):* `FlowExpert.precompute_memory()` reads the exact same `40` $\mathcal{Z}^{\text{geo}}$ tokens (dropping raw 2D visual tokens by `50%` during training), while `detach_backbone=True` on continuous motor noise + `10%` online Spatial-QA slot replay insulates the backbone.
     - *`navrun` (`R2R-CE`):* The navigation policy selects a waypoint slot from $\mathcal{Z}^{\text{geo}}$ (combining semantic instruction match with metric 3D coordinates directly stored in the slot) and passes it to `controller_v2`.
3. **Why It Beats Standard VLM-to-VLA Transfer (5-Demo & `+Cam`):** Every dollar of Gemini API spatial-QA/4D data generation directly sharpens the exact `40` slots that `FlowExpert` and `navrun` consume—eliminating the representation gap between "knowing where the bowl is in VSI-Bench" and "reaching the bowl in `LIBERO-Spatial`." Simultaneously, constraining VLA updates through the slot interface prevents catastrophic spatial forgetting (`< 0.5 pt` VSI/ReVSI drop) and enables **positive backward transfer** from robot interaction back to physical 3D QA.
4. **Decisive Fast Proof Test & Kill Criterion:** Train Stage-2 `Spatial-V2` with `GeoControlSlots` (`K=40`) on K1 + `Point4D` + VSI/ReVSI, then run paired `5-demo` `LIBERO-Spatial` + `LIBERO-Plus +Cam` AND re-evaluate `VSI-dev` / `ReVSI-dev` after `10k` VLA steps. **Kill if** slot-bottlenecked `FlowExpert` trails unbottlenecked `U1` by `> 2 pp` on `LIBERO-Spatial`, fails to beat `B1` by `>= +10 pp` on `5-demo`, or loses `> 1.0 pt` on `ReVSI-dev` after VLA fine-tuning.
