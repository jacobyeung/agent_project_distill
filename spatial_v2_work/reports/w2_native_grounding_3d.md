# Native 3D Grounding & Persistent Cross-View Instance Slots (`SlotGeo-3D`)

**Lane:** `bluesky28/w2_native_grounding_3d` | **Date:** 2026-11-28 | **Target:** `<150 ms` Self-Contained 3D Grounding & Memory for Spatial QA + VLA

## Executive Thesis
SPLIT `X1` + `X6` established a hard physical law: **grounding, metric 3D geometry, and cross-view instance identity bind in series** (`+16.1 pp` jointly on VSI-Bench vs `+2.8` geometry-only and `+0.5` grounding-only; stripping cross-view identity collapses multi-instance accuracy `-14.6 pp`). In our 9B student, `K1-X` and `J1` proved the identical bottleneck:
- Swapping in GT depth + GT pose under deployed `SAM 3` masks yields **`-0.46 pp`** (`K1-X`), whereas GT masks unlock **`+13.8 pp` on `dev-S1`** (`+17.2 pp` on `K1`).
- Feeding a **tight 2D box with zero text into frozen `SAM 3` (`J1` arm `k`) recovers `+11.0 pp` (`80%` of the GT-mask ceiling)**.
- Yet serving external `SAM 3` (`0.85B`, `80 ms/frame` + `64 ms/prompt`) and `G3T` (`1.2B`, `1.5–25 s`) breaks `<150 ms` VLA control and fails `21/23` ARKit clips.

**`SlotGeo-3D`** deletes external `SAM 3` and heuristic 2D fragment linking at inference, replacing them with a **single-checkpoint, `<135 ms` (32f VQA) / `<55 ms` (5-view VLA) 3D grounding and persistent instance-slot engine**:
1. **Visual-Geometric Feature Pyramid (`F_geo`):** Pre-merger Qwen3.5 ViT taps (`stride 14`) fused with fast metric depth, spatial depth-discontinuity gradients, and gravity-leveled $SE(3)$ poses (`DA3-Metric` distilled into a `300M` `GeoTrunk` for VQA; forward-kinematics-locked for VLA).
2. **`GeoBoxRoI-3D` Head (`26.4M` params, `<12 ms`):** LM-Layer-16 noun-span cross-attention predicting IoU-calibrated objectness, 2D boxes, **`28×28` depth-gated RoI foreground masklets** (distilled offline from `J1-k`), border-truncation flags, 3D yaw, and VLA grasp/place keypoints.
3. **3D-Metric Cross-View Instance Slots (`<=32` slots, `<6 ms`):** Eroded Depth-Tukey lifting into a gravity-aligned 3D world (`<=2,048` scene voxels) clustered via **Same-Frame Must-Not-Link (`MNL`) + drift-aware 3D Mahalanobis + `SupCon` appearance embeddings**, emitting multi-view voxel-voted OBBs, `64`-pt surface clouds, counts, `first_seen_frame`, and any-frame `@frame k` egocentric polar coordinates.
4. **Shared-ViT Foveated Re-Read (`Zoom-and-Verify`, `~18 ms`):** Deterministic 4-crop `336×336` zoom with exact pinhole intrinsics warp $K_t \to K^{\text{crop}}_t$ whenever targets span `<=4` cells or show depth/identity ambiguity.

## 2. Architectural Comparison & Fused `SlotGeo-3D` Design

### 2.1 Comparing Candidate 3D Grounding Paradigms (Why Fusion Wins)
| Paradigm | Representative | Fatal Flaw Alone | What `SlotGeo-3D` Fuses from It |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Monocular Direct 3D Box** | Qwen3.5 `bbox_3d` JSON / Cube-RCNN | Focal-depth ambiguity ($Z/f$) causes `0.21 m` center bias (`0.27–0.57 m` cross-view jitter $\approx$ `0.58 m` chair gap) and class-median size memorization (`46.8` MRA `<0.4 m`). | Canonical **object front-axis / continuous $(\sin\psi,\cos\psi,\sin 2\psi,\cos 2\psi)$ heading prior** + pretrained open-vocab 2D box initialization. |
| **2D Box $\to$ External `SAM 3`** | `J1` Candidate 3 (`OWNHEAD` + `SAM 3`) | `SAM 3` image encoder adds `80 ms/frame` (`400 ms` on 5 VLA views; `2.56 s` on 32f VQA) + `0.85B` params, violating `<150 ms` control. | Offline **`J1-k` teacher rasterization** to supervise a `0.9M`-param, `<2 ms` **`28×28` Depth-Gated RoI Masklet Head**. |
| **Soft Voxel Attention Ops** | Pure `<q_locate>` over 3D feature cloud | Softmax over `2,048` voxels smears multi-instance attention across all chairs, placing $\boldsymbol{\mu}$ in empty mid-room air and losing per-object grasp extents. | **Sparse `<=2,048` 3D Scene Voxel Cloud $\mathcal{V}$** for floor/wall layout and raycast occlusion, paired with **discrete `MNL` Instance Slots** for crisp per-object OBBs. |

### 2.3 Pillar 1 — Visual-Geometric Pyramid & `GeoBoxRoI-3D` Head (`26.4M` Params)
1. **Pre-Merger Geometric Feature Pyramid (`F_geo`):**
   - Tap Qwen3.5 ViT *before* the $2\times 2$ spatial merger at blocks $\{8, 16, 24, \text{last}\}$ (`stride 14` $\Rightarrow$ `23×30 = 690` tokens/frame vs `130` merged), fused via `1×1` lateral projections into `384-d`.
   - Append **10 pixel-aligned geometric channels** from Depth+Pose: gravity-leveled Plücker ray $(\mathbf{d}_t, \mathbf{o}_t \times \mathbf{d}_t) \in \mathbb{R}^6$, metric log-depth $\log \hat{D}_t$, **depth-edge gradients** $(\partial_u \log \hat{D}_t, \partial_v \log \hat{D}_t)$ (popping out white radiators on white walls, plates on white tables, and glass edges), and epistemic $\hat{\sigma}_{D,t}$.
   - **Fast, ARKit-Safe Geometry Backing:**
     - *VLA (`U1-Native`, `5` views `[wrist_t, agent_t, wrist_{t-8,t-16,t-32}]`):* Wrist poses $T_{\text{base}\leftarrow\text{wrist}}(t)$ come **analytically from robot forward kinematics (`0.00°`, `6e-8 m`)**; `T_base_agent` locks at episode frame 0; shared-tap metric depth (`9 ms`) anchors scale to FK (`|s/s_GT - 1| <= 2.4e-7`). External `ViewSetG3T` (`2.1 s`) is deleted.
     - *Uncalibrated VQA (`32–64` frames):* Stage 1 swaps fragile `G3T` (`21/23` ARKit failures) for **`DA3-Nested`** (`1.78°` rot, `6/144` ARKit `>15°`, native metric `76.9` room MRA); Stage 2 distils `DA3` via **`Spatial-Forcing²`** (patch pointmaps + cross-frame Gram $\mathcal{L}_{\text{gram}}$ + 3-step damped Gauss-Newton BA `<12 ms`) into a **`300M` 6-layer Bidirectional `GeoTrunk`** (`52 ms` on 32f bf16), preserving frozen `OwnDepthScale` / `DA3` known-$K$ calibration.
2. **Span-Conditioned Proposal, Masklet & Affordance Outputs:**
   - Pool **LM Layer-16** noun-span states $\mathbf{E}_{\text{span}}$ (plus a Layer-0 lexical skip so rare indoor synonyms never wash out) into $\mathbf{q} \in \mathbb{R}^{384}$, broadcast across $M=8$ learnable instance queries $\{\mathbf{q} + \mathbf{o}_m\}_{m=1}^8$, and run 2 bidirectional query-patch cross-attention layers (`d=384`).
   - Emit decoupled per-proposal outputs:
     - **IoU-Calibrated Quality $z^{\text{obj}}_{t,m}$ & Frame Presence $z^{\text{pres}}_t$:** Supervised with **Varifocal Loss** against true $\text{IoU}(\hat{\mathbf{b}}, \mathbf{b}^*)$—never multiplied by `alpha=0.25` focal scalars. All proposals with $\sigma(z^{\text{obj}}_{t,m}) \ge 0.35$ enter 3D slot verification.
     - **2D Box $\hat{\mathbf{b}}_{t,m}$** + **4-Edge Truncation Flags $\hat{\mathbf{c}}^{\text{trunc}}_{t,m} \in [0,1]^4$** (downweighting image-clipped axes during OBB extent fitting).
     - **`28×28` Depth-Gated RoI Masklet $\hat{M}_{t,m}$:** `RoIAlign` ($28\times 28$) on $\mathbf{F}_{\text{geo}}$ $\to$ 3-layer `3×3` conv (`0.9M` params, `<2 ms` batched).
     - **3D Yaw & 6-DoF VLA Affordance Keypoints:** Predicts $(\sin\psi,\cos\psi,\sin 2\psi,\cos 2\psi)$ in gravity-up frame, L2-normalized mask-pooled appearance $\mathbf{f}^{\text{app}}_{t,m} \in \mathbb{S}^{127}$, and object-local metric **grasp point $\Delta\mathbf{p}_{\text{grasp}}$** (handle/rim), **place surface center $\Delta\mathbf{p}_{\text{place}}$**, **approach axis $\hat{\mathbf{a}} \in \mathbb{S}^2$**, and **gripper width $w_{\text{jaw}}$**.

### 2.4 Pillar 2 — 3D-Metric Cross-View Instance Slots (Solving `X6`, Counting, Size & Occlusion)
1. **Eroded Depth-Tukey Lifting & `<=2,048` Scene Voxel Cloud:**
   - Erode $\hat{M}_{t,m} > 0.5$ by 1 RoI cell and filter interior depths around weighted median $D_{\text{med}}$: keep pixels with $|\hat{D}_t - D_{\text{med}}| \le 1.5\,\text{IQR}_D + 0.08\text{ m}$ and grazing $\cos\theta > 0.25$. Unproject to gravity-aligned `+Z` world coordinates $\mathbf{P}_{t,m}$, yielding robust centroid $\boldsymbol{\mu}_{t,m}$ and sensor+pose covariance $\Sigma^{\text{obs}}_{t,m}$.
   - Simultaneously scatter all clip patches into a sparse **3D Scene Voxel Grid $\mathcal{V}$ (`<=2,048` voxels; pitch `8 cm` room / `1.5 cm` tabletop)** tracking occupancy $\rho_v$ and floor plane $z_{\text{floor}} = P_5(Z_{\mathcal{V}})$ (directly powering `NU` navigation floor-waypoint lifting and raycast line-of-sight).
2. **Constrained `MNL` + 3D-Appearance Slot Clustering (Solving `0.58 m` Neighbor Collisions):**
   - Pure 3D distance clustering merges adjacent dining chairs (`42.5` MRA on `count >= 5`), whereas mask-appearance oracle hits **`80.6`**. Between observations $i=(t_1,m_1)$ and $j=(t_2,m_2)$, compute:
     $$A_{ij} = \mathbb{I}[t_1 \neq t_2] \cdot \exp\!\left(-\tfrac{1}{2}(\boldsymbol{\mu}_i - \boldsymbol{\mu}_j)^\top \big(\Sigma^{\text{obs}}_i + \Sigma^{\text{obs}}_j + \Sigma_{\text{drift}}(|t_1 - t_2|)\big)^{-1}(\boldsymbol{\mu}_i - \boldsymbol{\mu}_j)\right) \cdot \sigma\!\left(\frac{\langle \mathbf{f}^{\text{app}}_i, \mathbf{f}^{\text{app}}_j\rangle - \tau_{\text{app}}}{0.07}\right) \cdot \mathcal{S}_{\text{dim}}(i,j)$$
     **Hard Same-Frame Must-Not-Link ($\mathbb{I}[t_1 \neq t_2]$)** guarantees $K_{t^*}$ co-visible objects in frame $t^*$ establish $\ge K_{t^*}$ distinct slots regardless of trajectory drift, while $\Sigma_{\text{drift}}$ absorbs baseline pose error and $\mathbf{f}^{\text{app}} + \mathcal{S}_{\text{dim}}$ preserve cross-cut identity (`X6`). Constrained agglomerative/Sinkhorn clustering (`<3 ms`) yields persistent **3D Object Slots** $\{\mathcal{S}_k\}$.
3. **Multi-View Voxel Consensus, Ray Carving & Dual Interface:**
   - Accumulate slot points in a local voxel grid (`5 cm` room / `5 mm` VLA): keep voxels seen in $\ge \min(2, |\mathcal{S}_k|)$ views and **carve floating spikes** violated by free-space rays (`D_ray > D_voxel + 0.10 m`). Confirm $\mathcal{S}_k$ iff $\ge 2$ ray-consistent views agree OR 1 view exceeds $\sigma(z^{\text{obj}}) > 0.75$ (eliminating `h1`'s `1,098` absent-frame false positives).
   - Fit gravity-aligned **$\text{OBB}_k = (\mathbf{c}_k, L_k, W_k, H_k, \psi_k)$** (5th–95th Tukey percentiles along $\psi_k$, truncation-adjusted) + **`64`-pt FPS Surface Cloud $\mathcal{P}^{\text{surf}}_k$** for exact surface-to-surface gap $d_{\text{gap}}(A,B) = \min_{\mathbf{x}\in\mathcal{P}_A,\mathbf{y}\in\mathcal{P}_B}\|\mathbf{x}-\mathbf{y}\|_2$.
   - **Dual Interface:**
     1. *Continuous Latent Slot Prefix (`<=32` tokens at LM input + `tap 16/-1`):* Feeds gravity-leveled 3D slots directly into **direct VQA** (rel-dir, size, route) and **VLA (`QwenOFT` `Linear(36->8192)` + `FlowExpert` cross-attn)** with `0` tool calls.
     2. *Single-Shot `<scene_3d targets=[...] frame=k/>` Operator (`<20 ms`):* Replaces looping `<ground>`/`<gap>` cascades with one PyTorch call returning `count`, `first_seen_frame`, `obb_lwh_m`, `surface_gap_m`, and **any-frame `@frame k` egocentric `(horiz_dist_m, bearing_deg, rel_yaw_deg)`** via $\hat{T}^{-1}_{\mathcal{W}\leftarrow k}\mathbf{c}_k$—solving **`92.7%` out-of-view VSTI queries** (`0` visible pixels at frame $k$!).

### 2.5 Pillar 3 — Shared-ViT Foveated Re-Read ("Zoom-and-Verify") & Latency Budget
- **Deterministic VoI Triggers (Max $B_{\text{zoom}} = 4$ Crops/Query):** Fires iff (a) `0` confirmed slots (`max \sigma(z^{\text{obj}}) < 0.65`) $\to$ top-2 raw cross-attn peaks; (b) box `<2.0%` area (`<=4` stride-14 cells) or depth $\text{IQR}_D > 0.15\text{ m}$ (handles, glass, LIBERO ramekins) $\to$ top-2 widest-parallax views ($\Delta\theta \ge 15^\circ$); or (c) crowd ambiguity ($A_{ij} \in [0.35, 0.65]$).
- **Exact Projective Re-Lift:** Crop `336×336` (`2×` pad) from RAM frame buffers (`0` disk IO) and run one `B=4` micro-batch through **the shared Qwen3.5 ViT + `GeoBoxRoI-3D`** (`18 ms`), turning a `1`-cell speck into **`16–64` cells** (`78%–93%` hit zone). Update intrinsics analytically ($K^{\text{crop}}_t$) and affine-anchor crop depth $\hat{D}^{\text{hi}}$ to surrounding coarse metric depth $\hat{D}_t$ so global scale $s$ and pose $\hat{T}_{\mathcal{W}\leftarrow t}$ stay locked.

## 4. Top Confounds Priced Up-Front & Built-In Safeguards
1. **Scale–Extent Cancellation (`SPLIT S1–S2` Trap):** Fixing depth scale alone regresses size if single-view masks shrink extents `-20%`. *Safeguard:* Audit `median(s_pred / s_GT) ∈ [0.98, 1.02]` and `median(L_pred / L_GT) ∈ [0.95, 1.05]` separately in `KT-2` using ScanNet++ v1 `×2`-fixed boxes and truncation-compensated multi-view voxel voting.
2. **ARKit Trajectory Ill-Conditioning (`21/23` `G3T` Failures):** Grounding gains look muted on ARKit (`J1-k` `+1.8` vs `+15.8` SN++) under broken `G3T` poses (`g - e = +15.1` on ARKit). *Safeguard:* Report `SN/SN++` (`84`) and `ARKit` (`33`) separately, and pair `SlotGeo-3D` with `DA3-Nested` + 3-step damped Gauss-Newton BA (`KT-0c`).
3. **Zoom Procrastination & Crop Scale Illusion:** Unbounded zooming wastes latency; naive crop depth guesses `2×` closer range. *Safeguard:* Cap $B_{\text{zoom}} \le 4$ batched crops (`<=20 ms`), stop once $\sigma_{\mathbf{p}} \le 2\text{ cm}$, and enforce $K^{\text{crop}}_t$ Plücker rays + affine coarse-depth anchoring.
