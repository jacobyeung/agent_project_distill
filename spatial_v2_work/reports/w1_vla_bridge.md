# Spatial VLM → VLA Transfer Audit & Architectural Bridge (`w1_vla_bridge`)

## Executive Summary
Pure spatial-VQA text SFT leaves visual and LM hidden states geometry-blind (`~18–20°` pose direction plateau across `f17`, `C1-a`, `T0`), erodes 2D pixel pointing (`75.9% → 24.7%` in-mask on `f17`), and emits multi-second CoT traces unusable at 5–20 Hz robot control. Consequently, naive VQA-pretrained VLMs barely match vanilla bases on VLA tasks (`28/200` vs `29/200` in our Oct LIBERO pilot; InternVLA-M1 `98.0` vs `98.8` at full data). Turning our 9B spatial VLM into a sample-efficient backbone for manipulation, navigation, and embodied 3D control requires a **dual-pathway bridge**:
1. **Training-Time Representation Shaping (`B1` / `N1`, zero test-time overhead):** Inject and distil dense metric 3D pointmaps, SE(3) ego-motion (`S2`), and 3D object boxes into **mid-to-late LM visual patch states (`taps 16, 24, -1`)** via multi-layer G3T fusion (`GF-1`) + Spatial-Forcing-style patch alignment, and protect those features during VLA fine-tuning via detached-expert + `FAST` co-training (`F1`).
2. **Inference-Time Explicit 3D State & Waypoint Providers (`U1` / `NU` / Mobile Memory):** Pair a frozen multi-view provider (`U1`) with proprioceptive hand-eye scale calibration to feed camera-shift-invariant 3D `reach_base` vectors directly into the action head, and replace text dead-reckoning with native 2D floor-waypoint clicking (`NU`) + causal SE(3) submap stitching (`Ego-Exo4D` / `VQ3D`).

## 2. The Spatial-QA vs VLA Representation Gap

Standard VQA SFT optimizes scene-level multiple-choice/numeric text tokens and fails six concrete requirements of embodied control:

1. **Geometry Trapped in Text Tokens Instead of Continuous Hidden States:**
   - Qwen3.5's ViT encodes frames with `temporal_patch=2` and washes out camera motion in deeper blocks (linear/MLP pose probes stall at `~20°–83°` vs G3T `3.65°`; `IF-P0` vision-only LM branches cut pose error by only `2.6°` to `15.5°`). When `B1` drops auxiliary heads and `CarrierC1.generate` (`carrier_c1.py:308-348`) runs without tool calls, continuous heads (`QwenOFT`/`FlowExpert`) reading `tap 16` and `tap -1` receive 2D semantic features devoid of metric 3D structure.
2. **Erosion of Pixel Grounding & Missing 3D Affordance / Box Extent:**
   - Prose VQA SFT degraded `f17` 2D pointing from `75.9% → 24.7%` in-mask (`0/85` prose traces passed audit), while action-only VLA fine-tuning wipes out RefCOCO grounding within `20k` steps (StarVLA). Furthermore, our `Rank 1` and `K1` audits (`MODEL_IMPROVEMENT_PLAN_20261111.md`, `MASK_SOURCE_AFTER_TRACKER_DESIGN_20261122.md`) prove **whole-object masks and 3D boxes bind** (`+13.8` to `+17.2` pts from GT masks; `J1` GT-box oracle `k` recovers `+11.0` pts = `80%` of the mask ceiling; missing cuboid `X` and heading `H` block `1,359` VSI/ReSTI items). Manipulation needs sub-3 cm 3D grasp/place extents and orientation, not coarse centroids.
3. **High-Latency Multi-Turn Tool Text vs 5–20 Hz Closed-Loop Control:**
   - Emitting `500–2,000` CoT tokens or running full `FrozenG3TTower` (`18–25 s`/query) stalls closed-loop control and risks token-cap loops (`CP1` route-planning programs capped `8/20` items and dropped `58.0 → 26.0`; `E0` direct capped `10/25` displacement items). VLAs need `<0.15 s` single-forward hidden-state readouts (`QwenOFT`/`FlowExpert`) or amortized compact state (`U1` every `0.4 s`, `NU` single-line `<goal>` clicks).
4. **Multi-Camera Wrist + Third-Person (`Ego-Exo`) Calibration & Camera-Shift Invariance:**
   - Spatial VQA reads one continuous monocular scan in frame-1 coordinates. Manipulation feeds simultaneous wide-baseline `wrist_t + agentview_t` views and commands deltas in **robot-base axes**. Policies tied to third-person pixels collapse under camera shifts (`LIBERO-Plus`: OpenVLA-OFT `97.1 → 59.7`, $\pi_0$ `94.2 → 15.8`), whereas ego-to-exo registration on `Ego-Exo4D` (`n=210`) lifts accuracy `+44.6` pts (`39.3 → 83.9` on Suite 2A).
5. **Metric Scale Drift Across Synthetic Renders & Tabletop Ranges:**
   - VQA tolerates coarse relative priors (VSI hard direction is `84%` "front"). Control needs centimetre-exact reach (`0.1–1.5 m`) and step lengths (`0.25–2.0 m`). Monocular indoor depth heads fail out-of-domain (`V-C` depth `carrier_c1_decode.py:201-208` reads `1.94×` on Habitat; G3T raw translation is `0.46×`), requiring kinematic (`U1`) or floor-plane (`NU`) scale anchoring.
6. **Agent-Induced & Independent 3D Point / Gripper Flow (Dynamic Scenes):**
   - Moving robot arms, grasped objects riding the wrist camera, and walking humans violate static-scene bundle adjustment (`VGGT` Sintel ATE `0.167` vs $\pi_3$ `0.074`; static G3T trails `Point4D`/$\pi_3$ by `7–9` pts on `DSI-Bench`, while unanchored `Any4D` loses `-41.6` pts on static VSTI distance). VLAs require ego-motion-decoupled 3D point/gripper flow (`(frame_i, u, v) → X_{frame_j}`) anchored to static metric scale.

## 3. How Depth & Camera-Pose Heads Specifically Unlock VLAs

### 3.1 Training-Time Representation Shaping (Making Bare `B1` & `N1` Stronger Backbones)
Published ablations confirm training-only 3D losses reshape VLM hidden states: **Cambrian-P** gains `+4.7` VSI and `+20.0` VSTI camera movement from a pose head dropped at inference; **GeoVR** gains `+5.4` VSI from `pose + depth + scale + alignment` heads; **Spatial Forcing** aligns LM layer `24/32` patch tokens to VGGT during training and lifts OpenVLA-OFT LIBERO **`92.7 → 96.9`** (`42.3%` at `1%` data, `75.8%` at `5%` data). To maximize `B1`/`N1` transfer when heads are dropped:
1. **Upgrade from Sparse `CAM`-Carrier Supervision to Dense Patch-Level Pointmap Distillation (`GF-1` + `Taps 16, 24`):**
   - Currently, `CPDM` reads only `32` `CAM` carrier tokens (absent in `B1`) and `C1-r` aligned only camera tokens (`GX_GEOMETRY_INPUT_DESIGN_20261109.md:7`). Adding **`GF-1`** (multi-layer G3T bank injected into visual tokens via zero-init matrix) **plus a Spatial-Forcing-style 3D pointmap/feature alignment loss on LM layers `16` and `24` visual patch tokens** forces continuous metric 3D coordinates directly into the exact hidden-state taps (`16` and `-1`) read by `FlowExpert` cross-attention and `QwenOFT` `🔍` queries.
2. **Joint Cross-Frame SE(3) Pose (`S2`), G3T-Aligned Depth, & Own 3D/2D Box Head (`J1` Candidate 3):**
   - Supervising `S2` (pose targets on routing/video clips) cut own-head rotation error **`17.9° → 12.0°`** and lifted movement direction **`75.3% → 82.6%`** in `100` steps (`H1-1`) with zero direct harm. Combining `S2` + G3T-aligned dense depth (`log-sd 0.077 → 0.026`) + an **own box head** (`J1` GT-box oracle `+11.0` pts) forces Qwen's full-attention and DeltaNet states to track SE(3) camera motion and 3D object extents across `32–64` frames—directly pre-conditioning `N1` navigation and `B1` multi-view fusion.
3. **Knowledge-Insulated VLA Co-Training (`F1` + 10–20% Geometric Replay):**
   - Training `FlowExpert` with `detach_backbone: true` alongside `FAST` action tokens (`F1`) **plus 10–20% co-trained point-depth / pose / pointing batches** prevents action fine-tuning from erasing spatial representations (extending $\pi_{0.5}$'s semantic co-training to metric 3D).

### 3.2 Inference-Time Explicit 3D State, Pointmap & Pose Providers (`U1`, `NU`, & Mobile Control)
At `1–5` demos/task or `1–10%` nav data, explicit 3D readouts turn sample-hungry policy regression into coordinate-aligned control:
1. **Manipulation (`U1` Dual-Channel `Where` Provider):**
   - Frozen student presence (`AUROC 0.962`) + 2D pointing selects the right semantic object, `Sam3Tower` segments it, `ViewSetG3T` lifts eroded masks across `5` wrist+agent views, and proprio + `T_eef_cam` anchors exact metric scale to compute **`reach_base = x_target_base − x_eef_base`** in robot-base action axes.
   - Injecting `where` as both prompt text and head-side `Linear(36→8192)` (FALCON: head-side spatial tokens beat VLM-input `3.91` vs `3.79` on CALVIN and `87.5%` vs `60.0%` at `20` real demos) hands `QwenOFT` the exact 3D reach vector while guaranteeing **invariance to `LIBERO-Plus` third-person camera shifts**.
2. **Navigation (`NU` Visual Waypoint Clicking + Pose History):**
   - Clicking `<goal frame=32 x=u y=v>` and lifting via known camera height + gravity (`floor_plane`) bypasses `1.94×` render depth-scale drift and achieves an **88.7% SR** oracle-click ceiling, while `g3t_depth` checks sloped floors/obstacles and 32-frame pose heads prevent oscillation and resolve `R2RIE-CE` direction-word errors.
3. **Fast Native Own-Head Serving & Mobile Household Memory (`Ego-Exo4D` / `VQ3D` → `RoboCasa365`):**
   - Once the student's **own pose head (`<10°`), G3T-distilled depth head, and own box head** pass `P-1..P-4`, they replace external `ViewSetG3T + Sam3Tower` inside `WhereProvider`, cutting `U1` chunk latency from `~1.5–3.5 s` to **`<0.15 s`** in one self-contained 9B checkpoint (reinforceable end-to-end via `rlkit-v1`). On mobile manipulation (`RoboCasa365`), chaining pose + depth heads into causal SE(3) submap stores supplies persistent out-of-view 3D object memory (`VQ3D` proxy L2 `1.65 m → 0.43 m`, `Succ@1m` `32.7% → 73.7%`) and calibration-free multi-camera registration (`Ego-Exo4D` `+33.5` pts).
