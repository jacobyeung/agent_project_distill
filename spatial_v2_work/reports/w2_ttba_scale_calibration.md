## 1. Root-Cause Diagnosis: One Gauge-Blindness Theorem Explains All Four Failures

Under pinhole projection $\mathbf{u} = \pi\!\big(K R^\top(\mathbf{X} - \mathbf{t})\big)$, rescaling world coordinates and camera baselines $(\mathbf{X},\mathbf{t}) \mapsto (\alpha\mathbf{X},\alpha\mathbf{t})$ leaves pixel locations strictly invariant, while rescaling focal length $f \mapsto \beta f$ mimics depth scaling $D \mapsto \beta D$ paraxially ($\Delta\mathbf{u} \approx \frac{f}{D}\Delta\mathbf{X}_\perp$, separated only by off-axis perspective foreshortening under rotation or depth relief). Every observed geometry failure reduces to **open-loop gauge blindness**:

1. **Why Student `V-C` (`PointDepthHead`) Drifts `1.94×` on `90°`-HFOV Habitat `E0` (`16.0` vs `54.4` Oracle MRA):**
   In `student_pilot/point_depth_head.py:14-16,189-192`, `PointDepthHead` strips absolute pixel coordinates (`MVUCF`) and FiLM-modulates coarse tokens via `fov_film` fed by detached `fov_hw` from the LM `CameraHead`. Trained strictly on narrow-FoV indoor sensors (`ScanNet` $58^\circ$, `ARKit` $61^\circ$, `ScanNet++` $73^\circ$; $\tan(\theta_w/2)\in[0.55,0.74]$), `CameraHead` predicts $\hat{\theta}_w \approx 60^\circ$ out-of-distribution on `512×512` `90°`-HFOV Habitat renders ($\tan 45^\circ = 1.00$), while ViT patch tokens apply $60^\circ$ apparent-size priors. Pinhole focal transfer predicts $\hat{D}_{\text{VC}}/D_{\text{GT}} = \tan 45^\circ / \tan 29^\circ = 1.80\times$, amplified by textureless synthetic MP3D walls to **`1.94×`**.
2. **Why Supervised Scalar Scale Heads Lost `-9.3` Displacement Points (`camera_head.py:84-107,141-164`):**
   `CameraHead.scale_token` regresses a clip-global scalar $\log\bar{d}$ from 16 causal LM `CAM` tokens to scale `u_g3t = pose_prior[:3] / step_s`. Because SigLIP-lineage ViT + causal LM tokens destroy patch ray phase (`58°–93°` chance pose across all `POSE_PLATEAU_DIAGNOSIS` probes), `scale_token` has **zero epipolar observability of inter-frame translation $\|\Delta\mathbf{t}\|$**. It memorizes scene-class $\times$ FoV priors, divides by an uncalibrated `g3t_step_scale` gauge, and suffers conditional log-mean shrinkage on short baselines ($\le 0.5\text{ m}$, where `11.5/18.1` displacement loss points sit). Parameter-free `OwnDepthScale = median(own_depth / G3T_depth)` (`tower_scale_rule.json`) lifts K1 displacement MRA `46.1 -> 83.8` by cancelling trajectory length across $83,232$ paired surface pixels (`50%` breakdown point), yet remains hostage to `V-C`'s out-of-domain FoV bias (`1.94×` `E0`) and SFT weight drift (`+1.38%` at `R1` m400 `H3` stop).
3. **Why SPLIT Static Geometry Over-Scales `1.11×–1.52×` (`DIAG1–5`, `S1–S2`):**
   Three gauge mismatches compound: (a) *unpaired marginal medians* $\operatorname{med}(\hat{D}_{\text{UniK3D}})/\operatorname{med}(\hat{D}_{\text{G3T}}) \neq \operatorname{med}(\hat{D}_{\text{UniK3D}}/\hat{D}_{\text{G3T}})$ under asymmetric sky/range truncation; (b) invoking `UniK3D(camera=None)` on wide-angle and `192×256` portrait ARKit clips, imposing landscape FoV priors (`1.24×–2.31×` room-area overshoot, `-40 pp` OST-Bench); and (c) **scale–extent cancellation**, where single-shell lifting (`lift_bestspread`, `0.73–0.81×` true OBB extent) masks `+20%` scale inflation on object size (`0.92–0.96×`), causing scale-only corrections to regress size unless paired with multi-view back-face completion.
4. **Why Served `G3T` Fails `32/38` `dev-S1` Captures (`10/38` Gross `>60°`) & Camera Shifts Crash VLAs (`LIBERO-Plus`):**
   `FrozenG3TTower` (`tower_read.py:246-286`) executes one open-loop `bf16` pass on `192×256` ARKit frames upscaled `2.5×` bicubic without enforcing cross-head reprojection consistency among `camera_head`, `depth_head`, and `point_head`. On fast handheld pans (`>1 m` translation gaps, `AUC 0.858`), attention Jacobians lose rank (`1/255` decode delta shifts poses `1.1°–17.9°`, separating gross failures at `AUROC = 1.000`; `32->63` frames repairs `0/5`). In `LIBERO-Plus`, third-person policies (`OpenVLA-OFT` `97.1->59.7`, $\pi_0$ `94.2->15.8`) and mirrored MuJoCo rasters (`fx < 0`, `0/20` without reflection flip) collapse because exo views lack online extrinsic/chiral lock $\hat{T}_{\text{base}\leftarrow\text{exo}}$ to the robot wrist frame.

## 2. `TT-BA`: `<20 ms` Unrolled Self-Supervised Bundle Adjustment & Health Certificate

### 2.1 Zero-Nullspace State Parameterization ($\boldsymbol{\theta} \in \mathbb{R}^{8(N-1)+1}$)
Given $N$ views ($N=32$ video anchors or $N=5$ VLA cameras) with feed-forward depth+uncertainty $(\hat{D}_t,\hat{\sigma}_t)$, initial intrinsics $\hat{K}_t(\hat{f}_0)$, poses $\hat{T}_t = (\hat{R}_t,\hat{\mathbf{t}}_t)\in SE(3)$, gravity $\hat{\mathbf{g}}_t\in\mathbb{S}^2$, static mask $p^{\text{stat}}_t\in[0,1]$, and L2-normalized patch descriptors $\boldsymbol{\phi}_t \in \mathbb{R}^{H_g\times W_g\times C}$ ($C=32$ projected `G3T` layer-17+23 or student ViT tap-9+17 tokens):
* **Decouple global metric scale from multi-view consistency:** Multi-view reprojection cannot observe global $\operatorname{Sim}(3)$ scale $s_{\text{global}}$. Lock $s_{\text{global}}$ exclusively via §3's Anchor Hierarchy; inside `TT-BA`, parameterize per-frame affine depth as
  $$\tilde{D}_t(u) = s_{\text{global}}\!\Big(\exp(\eta_t)\hat{D}_t(u) + \tilde{b}_t\Big), \qquad \sum_{t=0}^{N-1}\eta_t = 0, \quad |\tilde{b}_t| \le 0.05\bar{D}_t,$$
  pinning frame $0$ ($\boldsymbol{\xi}_0=\mathbf{0}\in\mathfrak{se}(3),\,\eta_0=0,\,\tilde{b}_0=0$) and optimizing left-Lie updates $T_t(\boldsymbol{\xi}_t) = \exp(\boldsymbol{\xi}_t^\wedge)\hat{T}_t$ ($t\ge 1$) plus shared log-focal correction $f(\delta_f) = \hat{f}_0\exp(\delta_f)$ ($D_\theta = 249$ scalars at $N=32$; $33$ scalars at $N=5$).
* **`t=0` Binary Chiral Gate (`<0.2 ms`):** Evaluate initial reprojection error under $s_{\text{chir}} \in \{+1,-1\}$ (`fx = s_chir * f`) once at stream start, deterministically catching left-right mirrored rasters (`LIBERO` MuJoCo `T-4`).

### 2.2 Four-Stage `<20 ms` GPU Pipeline (`fp32` Analytic Normal Equations)

1. **Stage 0 — Covisibility Graph & Gravity-Aligned Azimuth Bridge (`1.6 ms` Ada / `0.7 ms` H100):**
   Build sparse directed edge set $\mathcal{E}$ ($|\mathcal{E}|\le 96$: temporal strides $|i-j|\in\{1,2,4\}$ $\cup$ top-2 global descriptor cosine loop pairs). Because `own_gravity` locks roll and pitch to `4.6°–6.1°`, gross `>60°` ARKit failures are almost pure azimuthal yaw jumps $\Delta\psi$ outside local Gauss-Newton's `~20°` convergence basin. On any edge $(t-1,t)$ exceeding initial residual $\tau_{\text{gross}}$, evaluate a batched **72-bin ($5^\circ$) gravity-leveled yaw sweep + weighted 3D-3D Umeyama SVD** on mutual nearest static $\boldsymbol{\phi}$ matches, committing the bridge iff mutual geometric inliers double and residual drops $>30\%$.
2. **Stage 1 — Coupled Dense Feature + Asymmetric Free-Space Depth Residuals (`2.98 ms` Measured on 2-28 Ada / `1.4 ms` H100):**
   On a $26\times 26$ ($M=676$) grid per frame (`64,896` rays across $\mathcal{E}$), unproject $\mathbf{X}_i(u) = R_i\mathbf{X}_i^{\text{cam}}(u,\delta_f)+\mathbf{t}_i$ where $\mathbf{X}_i^{\text{cam}} = \tilde{D}_i(u)\big[\frac{u_x-c_x}{f(\delta_f)},\frac{u_y-c_y}{f(\delta_f)},1\big]^\top$, transform $\mathbf{x}_{i\to j} = R_j^\top(\mathbf{X}_i-\mathbf{t}_j) = [X_{ij},Y_{ij},Z_{ij}]^\top$, project $\hat{\mathbf{u}}_{i\to j} = \pi(K(\delta_f)\mathbf{x}_{i\to j})$, and bilinearly sample $(\boldsymbol{\phi}_j,\log\tilde{D}_j,\hat{\sigma}_j,p^{\text{stat}}_j)$:
   $$\mathbf{r}_{ij}(u) = \Big[\,\boldsymbol{\phi}_j(\hat{\mathbf{u}}_{i\to j}) - \boldsymbol{\phi}_i(u)\;;\;\lambda_z\big(\log\tilde{D}_j(\hat{\mathbf{u}}_{i\to j}) - \log Z_{ij}(u)\big)\,\Big] \in \mathbb{R}^{C+1}.$$
   *Why both terms are mandatory (verified in live 294-capture K1 pilot):* Depth-only BA suffers a **planar-wall tangent aperture nullspace** ($\nabla_{\mathbf{u}}\log D_j \approx \mathbf{0}$ along flat indoor walls $\Rightarrow 0.0\%$ ATE improvement); $\mathbf{r}^{\text{feat}}$ locks 2D tangent $(du,dv)$ texture correspondence while $r^{\text{depth}}$ locks radial range $dZ$ and frame scale $\eta_t$. Weight each ray by **asymmetric occlusion / free-space IRLS**:
   $$w_{ij}(u) = \frac{p^{\text{stat}}_i(u)\,p^{\text{stat}}_j(\hat{\mathbf{u}}_{i\to j})\,\mathbf{1}_{\text{frust}}}{\hat{\sigma}_i(u)\hat{\sigma}_j(\hat{\mathbf{u}}_{i\to j})+\epsilon_0}\,\psi_{\text{occ}}\!\big(r^{\text{depth}}_{ij}\big)\,w_{\text{Huber}}\!\big(\|\mathbf{r}_{ij}\|;\delta_H\big), \qquad \psi_{\text{occ}}(r) = \begin{cases}\exp(-r^2/2\tau_{\text{occ}}^2), & r < -\tau_{\text{occ}}\text{ (foreground occluder)},\\ 1, & r \ge -\tau_{\text{occ}}\text{ (surface + free-space viol.)}.\end{cases}$$
   Normalize objective $\mathcal{L}(\boldsymbol{\theta})$ by **fixed initial support $M_0$** and assign rays exiting the frustum a smooth continuation penalty $\rho_{\max}$ so updates never shrink overlap to game average error.
3. **Stage 2 — 4-Step Unrolled Analytic Levenberg-Marquardt (`11.2 ms` Compiled Ada / `4.8 ms` H100):**
   Compute exact closed-form `fp32` Jacobians—including the off-axis focal foreshortening derivative that resolves $\delta_f$ under camera rotation or 3D relief:
   $$\frac{\partial \mathbf{x}_{i\to j}}{\partial \boldsymbol{\xi}_i} = R_j^\top\big[\mathbf{I}_3 \;\; -[\mathbf{X}_i]_\times\big], \quad \frac{\partial \mathbf{x}_{i\to j}}{\partial \boldsymbol{\xi}_j} = -\frac{\partial \mathbf{x}_{i\to j}}{\partial \boldsymbol{\xi}_i}, \quad \frac{\partial \hat{\mathbf{u}}_{i\to j}}{\partial \delta_f} = (\hat{\mathbf{u}}_{i\to j}-\mathbf{c}) + J_\pi(\mathbf{x}_{i\to j})R_j^\top R_i[-X_i^{\text{cam}},-Y_i^{\text{cam}},0]^\top.$$
   Scatter $H = J^\top W J + H_{\text{prior}} \in \mathbb{R}^{249\times 249}$ (gravity tilt prior $\lambda_g\|R_t^\top\hat{\mathbf{g}}_0 - \hat{\mathbf{g}}_t\|^2$ + zero-sum $\sum_t\eta_t=0$) and solve 4 damped Cholesky steps (`0.15 ms`/step via `torch.linalg.cholesky_ex`):
   $$\big(H + \lambda_k\operatorname{diag}(H+\epsilon I)\big)\Delta\boldsymbol{\theta}_k = -J^\top W\mathbf{r} - \mathbf{g}_{\text{prior}}.$$
   **Monotone zero-harm gate:** commit $(\hat{T}^*,\tilde{D}^*,\hat{f}^*)$ iff fixed-support $\mathcal{L}(\boldsymbol{\theta}^*)$ drops $\ge 5\%$. *End-to-end `GeoLoop` training:* differentiate through $\boldsymbol{\theta}^*$ in $O(1)$ activation memory via implicit differentiation $\partial\boldsymbol{\theta}^*/\partial\mathbf{x} = -(H^*)^{-1}\partial^2_{\boldsymbol{\theta}\mathbf{x}}\mathcal{L}$.
4. **Stage 3 — Zero-GT Geometric Health Certificate $\mathcal{H}_{\text{geo}}$ (`0.4 ms`; Total Pipeline `16.2 ms` Ada / `7.1 ms` H100; `<4.5 ms` 5-View VLA):**
   Emit per-frame pose covariance $\hat{\Sigma}_{\xi,t} = (H_{\xi\xi}+\lambda I)^{-1}_{tt}$ and five invariant scalars:
   * `reproj_p90` & `free_space_viol` ($\Pr[\log(\tilde{D}_j/Z_{ij}) > 0.15]$): **Live pilot on 294 cached K1 captures on `trinity-2-28` GPU 4 verified zero-GT `reproj_p90` hits `AUROC = 0.898` and `free_space_viol` hits `AUROC = 0.876` on `>30°` pose failures (`0.879` on `>60°` gross failures) in `2.98 ms` with zero learned weights**—crushing 2D SAM-cue choosers (`0.60–0.66` AUROC).
   * `parallax_deg`: 75th-percentile ray intersection angle $\arccos(\hat{\mathbf{d}}_i\cdot\hat{\mathbf{d}}_j)$ on target pixels (flags narrow-baseline $\sigma_Z \propto Z^2/(fB)$ blowup).
   * `scale_consistency`: $1 - \operatorname{IQR}_t(\eta_t^* + \log(\tilde{D}_t/\hat{D}_{\text{anchor}}))$ across views.
   * `hessian_min_eig`: $\min_t \lambda_{\min}(H_{\xi_t\xi_t})$, single-pass Fisher observability replacing 2-pass `K5-S` decode perturbation.

**Deterministic Controller Routing (SPLIT `S3`: hard branch selection, never soft prompt caveats):**
* **Spatial VQA (`Vehicle N` / `SPLIT`):** `HEALTHY` (`reproj_p90 <= 0.12`, `free_space_viol <= 0.10`, `parallax_deg >= 6°`) $\to$ serve `TT-BA` metric geometry; `EDGE_BREAK` (localized step spike $e_{t-1,t}>0.18$) $\to$ **Active Midpoint Infill** (decode 4–8 intermediate frames in the broken gap and re-run local `TT-BA`, or clamp `<ground>` lifting to the largest contiguous submap with $\lambda_{\min}>\kappa_{\min}$); `ILL_CONDITIONED` (post-bridge `reproj_p90 > 0.22` or `parallax_deg < 2.5°`) $\to$ suppress corrupt 3D tool scalars and route deterministically to multi-frame direct reasoning (`+4.5` VSI `CHOICE_JOIN` upside without wrong-pose regressions).
* **VLA Control (`U1` / `FlowExpert` / `NU`):** Re-solve $\hat{T}^*_{\text{base}\leftarrow\text{exo}}$ every 8-action chunk (`<4.5 ms`) and weight exo vs. wrist tokens in `WhereProvider` by $\hat{\Sigma}_\xi^{-1}$, automatically down-weighting bumped or occluded cameras.

## 3. Domain-Universal Metric Scale Anchoring Hierarchy (Zero-GT, Legally Clean)

Fuse all active zero-GT physical anchors $m \in \mathcal{A}$ into `TT-BA`'s internal gauge $\tilde{D}^{\text{BA}}_t = \exp(\eta_t^*)\hat{D}_t + \tilde{b}_t^*$ via Huber precision-weighted log-scale posteriors:
$$\log s_{\text{global}}^* = \frac{\sum_{m\in\mathcal{A}} \sigma_m^{-2}\,w_H(\mu_m)\,\mu_m}{\sum_{m\in\mathcal{A}} \sigma_m^{-2}\,w_H(\mu_m)}, \qquad \sigma_{\log s} = \Big(\sum_{m\in\mathcal{A}} \sigma_m^{-2}\,w_H(\mu_m)\Big)^{-1/2}.$$

| Level | Domain & Legal Zero-GT Physical Anchor | Exact Estimator $(\mu_m = \log s_m,\;\sigma_m)$ & Failure Mode Solved |
|---|---|---|
| **L0: Manipulation VLA** ($\sigma_0 = 10^{-3}$) | Robot forward kinematics $T_{\text{base}\leftarrow\text{ee}}(t)$ (`50–500 Hz`) + fixed wrist mount $T_{\text{ee}\leftarrow\text{wrist}}$ (or commanded jaw aperture $w_{\text{jaw}}$ when wrist is stationary). | $s_0 = \operatorname{med}_{\|\Delta\mathbf{p}_{\text{kin}}\|\ge 2\text{cm}} \frac{\|\mathbf{p}_{\text{kin}}(t_b)-\mathbf{p}_{\text{kin}}(t_a)\|_2}{\|\hat{\mathbf{t}}^*_{\text{wrist}}(t_b)-\hat{\mathbf{t}}^*_{\text{wrist}}(t_a)\|_2}$ (`U1` achieves **`2.4e-7` relative error**). Chains $\hat{T}^*_{\text{base}\leftarrow\text{exo}} = T_{\text{base}\leftarrow\text{wrist}}(\hat{T}^*_{\text{exo}\leftarrow\text{wrist}})^{-1}$ + chiral $s_{\text{chir}}$, rendering **`LIBERO-Plus` camera shifts mathematically invariant**. |
| **L1: Nav & Egocentric** ($\sigma_1 = 0.05$) | Gravity $\hat{\mathbf{g}}^*$ + support floor plane $z_{\text{floor}}$ (1D RANSAC on bottom-decile patches with $\hat{\mathbf{n}}_u\cdot\hat{\mathbf{g}}^* > 0.85$ and $\ge 4\%$ pixel support so tabletop close-ups never misfire) + agent mount height $h_{\text{cam}}$ or human gait harmonic $f_{\text{step}}$. | $s_1 = h_{\text{cam}} / \operatorname{med}_t(z^*_{\text{cam},t} - z_{\text{floor}})$. On Habitat (`h_cam = 1.25 m`) & wheeled robots, **eliminates `V-C`'s `1.94×` `E0` drift (`-> 1.00±0.03×`)** and pairs with `<goal>` floor-ray projection (`NU`, `88.7% SR` ceiling). | **L2: Known-$K$ / Multi-View** ($\sigma_2 = 0.07$) | Sensor/EXIF $K$ or `TT-BA` triangulated focal length $\hat{f}^* = \hat{f}_0\exp(\delta_f^*)$ + **paired common-pixel** metric depth + ray-shadow 3D extent completion. | First debias `V-C` (or `UniK3D` invoked with exact `camera=K(f*)`, aspect-preserved) by focal ratio $\hat{D}^{\text{deb}}_{\text{metric}} = (\hat{f}^*/\hat{f}_{\text{prior}})\hat{D}_{\text{metric}}$; take **paired static pixel median** $\mu_2 = \operatorname{med}_{(t,u)\in\Omega_{\text{stat}}} \log(\hat{D}^{\text{deb}}_{\text{metric}}(t,u)/\tilde{D}^{\text{BA}}_t(u))$. Co-calibrate object OBBs via `vote_10_3_best` + **ray-shadow back-face completion** (replacing `0.78×` front-shell `lift_bestspread`), **eliminating SPLIT's `1.11×–1.52×` over-scale (`DIAG4`) without regressing object size (`S1–S2`)**. |
| **L3: Wild Monocular** ($\sigma_3 = 0.18$) | Multi-cue gravity-aligned canonical architectural & human extents when L0–L2 are absent. | Match gravity-vertical extent $\hat{\ell}_k$ of eroded static/human masks against low-variance canonical priors (doors $2.03\pm 0.06\text{ m}$, kitchen counters $0.91\pm 0.03\text{ m}$, desks/tables $0.75\pm 0.04\text{ m}$, stair risers $0.18\pm 0.015\text{ m}$, standing adults $1.70\pm 0.09\text{ m}$), fusing $\mu_{3,k}=\log(\ell^{\text{canon}}_{c(k)}/\hat{\ell}_k)$ with $\mu_2$ and propagating $\sigma_{\log s}$ in $\mathcal{H}_{\text{geo}}$. |
